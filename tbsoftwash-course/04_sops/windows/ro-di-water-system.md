# RO/DI Water System for Window Cleaning (Build Overview)

Asset: `07_assets/ro-diagram/image_0_624x453.png`

## Diagram components (as shown)
1) Water spigot
2) Carbon filter
3) Reverse osmosis membrane (stage 1)
4) Rejection water valve (waste/concentrate control)
5) Reverse osmosis membrane (stage 2)
6) Deionized water tank
7) Water fed pole

## What it does
- Carbon removes chlorine (protects membranes)
- RO removes most dissolved solids
- DI polishes remaining TDS for spot-free glass
