# House Wash (Soft Wash) SOP v2 (Operator)

## Service
Exterior house wash using **soft wash** methods (low pressure) for:
- vinyl siding
- stucco
- painted surfaces
- soffits/fascia/trim

> Operator principle (TBSoftWash standard): **Don’t blast houses.** Pressure is for controlled rinsing only when appropriate. Chemistry + dwell does the cleaning.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Organic growth and general soil removed to the agreed standard without damaging finishes.
- Landscaping protected and left healthy.
- No residue left on windows/screens/doors/fixtures.
- Water intrusion avoided (vents, soffit gaps, failing seals).
- Proof Pack complete + delivered.

## Safety / PPE
- Sealed eye protection + chemical-resistant gloves.
- Long sleeves/pants.
- Slip-resistant footwear.
- Respiratory protection when needed (mist/wind).
- Ladder safety if ladders are used.

## Pre-checks (arrival)
### 1) Scope confirmation
- Confirm surfaces and what’s included (walls only vs soffits/fascia/trim).
- Confirm exclusions:
  - oxidation restoration (separate process)
  - hard-water staining (separate process)
  - rust stains (separate process)

### 2) Quick surface diagnosis (decision tree)
**A) Organic growth** (green/black mildew/algae)
- Yes → standard soft wash process.

**B) Oxidation** (chalky residue, color rubs off on towel)
- Yes → set expectations. Standard wash may not “fix” oxidation.

**C) Rust / hard-water / irrigation staining**
- Yes → document and quote as separate specialty treatment.

### 3) Pre-existing condition photos (CYA)
Photograph before any change:
- peeling paint
- cracked stucco
- failing caulk
- loose fixtures
- existing stains/etching

### 4) Water/electrical/hazards
- Identify outlets, cameras, exterior lighting.
- Plan hose routing and runoff.

### 5) Plant protection plan (mandatory)
- Pre-wet delicate landscaping.
- Stage dedicated rinse line.
- Plan continuous rinse cycles during dwell.

Reference: `02_chemicals/pages/landscaping-protection.md`

### 6) Customer expectation setting (2 minutes)
- “Soft wash cleans safely. Pressure is not the cleaner.”
- “We protect landscaping and rinse throughout.”
- “We document before/after.”
- If oxidation: “We can improve it, but oxidation is not the same as mildew.”

## Tools / equipment
- Soft wash system (rig-based) with controllable application.
- Dedicated rinse hose/nozzle.
- Extension poles/wands.
- Selective masking/tarping.
- Soft brush for limited agitation (as needed).
- Towels for detail wipe-down.

No public recipes.

- Use surfactant/adhesion as appropriate.
- Control overspray.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Setup & protection
1. Start plant pre-wet.
2. Selectively cover sensitive areas if needed.
3. Confirm wind direction.
4. Stage rinse line for constant use.

### 2) Apply (soft wash)
1. Work top-down (soffits/fascia → walls).
2. Even coverage; avoid striping.
3. Avoid spraying directly into vents, soffit gaps, and failing seals.

### 3) Dwell + monitor
1. Allow dwell.
2. Rinse plants during dwell.
3. Watch runoff patterns.

### 4) Rinse (controlled)
1. Rinse thoroughly.
2. Check windows, doors, and fixtures for residue.
3. Final rinse landscaping.

### 5) Detail + touch-ups
1. Re-hit stubborn organic spots if safe.
2. Use minimal agitation (soft brush) when needed.

### 6) Proof Pack
Follow: `04_sops/proof-pack/proof-pack-sop-v1.md`

## Quality checks (QA)
- Even results in shade + sun.
- **Glass check:** no residue/spotting on windows.
- **Fixtures check:** no residue on lights, door hardware, cameras.
- No residue at windows/screens/doors.
- No drip streaks on paint.
- Plants healthy after final rinse.

## Common failure modes
- Oxidation mistaken for mildew → prevention: diagnosis + expectations.
- Striping from uneven application.
- Plant stress from insufficient rinsing.
- Water intrusion from blasting vents/gaps.
