# House Wash (Soft Wash) SOP v1 (Operator)

> Superseded by: `04_sops/houses/house-wash-softwash-sop-v2.md`

## Service
House wash / exterior wall wash using **soft wash** methods (low pressure) designed for:
- vinyl siding
- stucco
- painted surfaces
- soffits/fascia
- trim

> Operator principle: **Do not “blast” house washing.** Pressure is for rinsing only (when appropriate). Let chemistry + dwell do the cleaning.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Organic growth, dirt, and visible streaking are removed to the agreed standard without surface damage.
- Landscaping is protected and left healthy.
- Windows, screens, doors, and painted surfaces are not left with residue.
- Proof Pack complete: before/during/after photos + any pre-existing conditions documented.

## Safety / PPE
- Sealed eye protection + chemical-resistant gloves.
- Long sleeves/pants.
- Slip-resistant footwear.
- Respiratory protection when needed (mist/wind).
- Ladder safety if ladders are used.

Heat protocol:
- Follow: `04_sops/safety/heat-sun-hydration.md`

## Pre-checks (arrival)
### 1) Scope verification + “what’s actually dirty?”
- Confirm surfaces: vinyl vs stucco vs painted vs raw wood.
- Identify oxidation vs organic growth (oxidation is not “mildew”).
- Identify heavy traffic zones: north sides, shaded walls, behind landscaping.
- Photograph pre-existing:
  - peeling paint
  - cracked stucco
  - failing caulk
  - rust marks
  - oxidation

### 2) Water/electrical/hazards
- Confirm water source, hose routing, and where rinse water will flow.
- Identify outlets, lights, doorbell cams, and any exposed wiring.

### 3) Plant protection plan (mandatory)
- Pre-wet delicate landscaping.
- Plan continuous rinse cycles during application/dwell.
- Identify any sensitive areas where runoff may concentrate.

### 4) Customer expectation setting (2 minutes)
- “We use a soft-wash process to clean safely. Pressure is not the cleaner.”
- “We protect landscaping and rinse throughout.”
- “We document before/after.”

## Tools / equipment
- Soft wash system (12V/air/etc. per rig) with control over application strength.
- Dedicated rinse hose/nozzle.
- Downstream injector (optional, depending on rig and surface).
- Extension poles/wands.
- Tarps/plastic for selective protection.
- Basic hand tools: brushes for detail spots (as needed), towels.

## Chemicals (ranges + why) — operator-only
We do not publish exact recipes in public-facing materials.

Operator guidance:
- Identify the soil type (organic vs oxidation vs rust vs hard water).
- For standard house wash, aim for effective dwell on organics with minimal mechanical force.
- For oxidation: set expectations; do not promise what you can’t safely deliver.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Setup
1. Start plant pre-wet.
2. Protect/cover as needed (selective masking).
3. Confirm wind direction.
4. Stage rinse line for constant use.

### 2) Apply (soft wash)
1. Work top-down (soffits/fascia → walls → lower sections).
2. Keep application even; avoid heavy “striping.”
3. Keep product off surfaces that should not be hit (per policy).

### 3) Dwell + monitor
1. Allow dwell.
2. Rinse plants during dwell.
3. Watch for runoff patterns; adjust.

### 4) Rinse (gentle)
1. Rinse thoroughly.
2. Check windows, doors, and trim for residue.
3. Final rinse of landscaping.

### 5) Detail + touch-ups
1. Re-hit stubborn spots if safe.
2. Use minimal mechanical agitation as needed (soft brush) — not pressure.

### 6) Proof Pack
- Repeat before angles; capture detail wins and any exceptions.

## Quality checks (QA)
- Verify even results in shade and sunlight.
- Confirm no residue at:
  - windows/screens
  - door thresholds
  - light fixtures
  - painted soffits
- Confirm plants look healthy.

## Common failure modes
### 1) Oxidation mistaken for “dirt”
- Prevention: explain oxidation; set realistic expectations.

### 2) Streaking / uneven application
- Prevention: consistent overlap pattern, controlled dwell.

### 3) Plant stress
- Prevention: pre-wet + continuous rinse + runoff control.

### 4) Water intrusion concerns
- Prevention: avoid spraying directly into vents, soffit gaps, failing seals.

## Customer communication
### Before
- “Soft wash cleans safely. Pressure is not the cleaner.”

### After
- Send completion message + proof photos.
- If any oxidation/staining remains: explain why and offer options within policy.
