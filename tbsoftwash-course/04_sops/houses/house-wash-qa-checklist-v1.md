# House Wash — QA Checklist (1 page)

## Before
- [ ] Scope confirmed (walls/soffits/trim)
- [ ] Oxidation vs organic diagnosed
- [ ] Pre-existing conditions photographed
- [ ] Plants pre-wet
- [ ] Wind checked
- [ ] Dedicated rinse line staged

## During
- [ ] Top-down workflow
- [ ] Even application (no striping)
- [ ] Avoid vents/soffit gaps/failing seals
- [ ] Plants rinsed during dwell

## Rinse / finish
- [ ] Thorough rinse
- [ ] Windows/doors/fixtures checked for residue
- [ ] Final plant rinse

## Proof Pack
- [ ] Before/after angles matched
- [ ] Exceptions documented (oxidation, hard water, rust)
- [ ] Proof Pack link logged

## Customer closeout
- [ ] Completion message sent
- [ ] Expectations set for anything outside scope
