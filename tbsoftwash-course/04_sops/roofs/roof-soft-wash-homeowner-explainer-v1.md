# Roof Soft Wash — Homeowner Explainer (Safety + What to Expect)

This page is homeowner-safe. It explains the process without publishing chemical recipes.

## Why we don’t pressure wash roofs
Roofs aren’t driveways.

High pressure can:
- lift or damage shingles
- force water where it shouldn’t go
- shorten roof life

A safe roof clean is about the right method, not brute force.

## What “soft washing” means
Soft washing uses:
- the right cleaning solution (chosen for the roof material)
- dwell time (letting it work)
- a gentle rinse

## How we protect your landscaping
Landscaping protection is part of the job:
- we pre-wet plants
- we rinse throughout
- we do a final rinse when we’re done

## What we document (Proof Pack)
You’ll receive before/after photos so you can see exactly what changed.

We also document any pre-existing damage we see (cracked tiles, missing shingles, prior repairs) before we start.

## What results to expect
Most roofs show dramatic improvement the same day.

In some cases, certain staining can continue to improve after rain or over time.

We’ll set expectations before we begin.

## Your part
- keep pets inside during service
- tell us about any sensitive plants or special concerns
- point out any known roof issues up front
