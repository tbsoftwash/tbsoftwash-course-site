# SOP — Gutter Cleaning (Springboard Service) (v1)

> Superseded by: `04_sops/gutters/gutter-cleaning-sop-v2.md`

## Goal (definition of done)
- Gutters are cleared, downspouts flow, work area is clean, and the customer receives a Proof Pack.

## Safety / PPE
- Gloves, eye protection
- Ladder safety (stabilizer, 3 points of contact)
- Avoid working in storms/high winds

## Tools / equipment
- Ladder + stabilizer
- Gutter scoop / trowel
- Bucket or bag system
- Hose w/ spray nozzle
- Optional: wet/dry vac (for certain situations)

## Step-by-step workflow
1) Arrival + walkthrough + identify downspout locations
2) Proof Pack “before” photos (overflow points, debris buildup)
3) Set ladder safely; protect landscaping as needed
4) Remove debris by hand/scoop into bucket/bag
5) Flush gutters to confirm flow
6) Clear clogs at downspouts (as possible)
7) Final rinse + cleanup (no mess)
8) Proof Pack “after” photos + completion message

## QA checklist
- No visible debris in gutters
- Downspouts draining
- No debris left on roof/ground
- Water test confirms flow

## Common failure modes
- Making a mess below gutters
- Not confirming downspout flow
- Ladder placement damage

## Customer communication
- Explain what was found (pine straw, shingle grit)
- Recommend cadence (2x/year typical; more with pines)

## Upsells / bundles
- Gutter brightening
- Roof soft wash / house wash
- Drain jetting (if buried drain lines)
