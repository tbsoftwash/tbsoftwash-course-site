# Gutter Cleaning SOP v2 (Springboard Service)

## Service
Gutter debris removal + downspout flow verification.

> Operator principle: Gutters are a **trust-builder** service. Don’t rush it. Don’t make a mess. Document what you found.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Gutters are cleared to the agreed standard.
- Downspouts are verified flowing (or clearly documented if not).
- Work area is clean (no debris left behind).
- Proof Pack completed and delivered.

## Safety / PPE
- Gloves + eye protection.
- Ladder safety: stabilizer where possible, 3 points of contact, no overreach.
- Heat protocol: `04_sops/safety/heat-sun-hydration.md`
- Avoid storms/high winds.

## Pre-checks (arrival)
1) Walk the home and identify:
   - downspout locations
   - known overflow points
   - landscaping and sensitive areas below
   - roof zones where debris will fall (valleys)
2) Scope confirmation:
   - “clean gutters” (debris removal) vs “brightening” (tiger stripe/oxidation restoration)
3) Proof Pack BEFORE photos (see Proof Pack SOP):
   - overflow staining
   - heavy debris sections
   - downspout outlets

## Tools / equipment
- Ladder + stabilizer
- Scoop/trowel + bucket/bag system
- Hose + spray nozzle
- Optional: wet/dry vac (select cases)
- Optional: downspout flush tool (when appropriate)

## Step-by-step workflow
1) **Set protection**
   - Pre-wet delicate plants under work zone.
   - Stage debris containment (bucket/bag) so nothing hits lawns/patios.

2) **Clean gutters**
   - Work in sections.
   - Remove debris into containment (don’t throw it).

3) **Flush & verify**
   - Flush each section to check for standing water.
   - Verify downspout discharge at the outlet.

4) **Downspout issues**
   - If flow is restricted: document and attempt safe clearing within policy.
   - If buried drains are suspected: document and recommend next step (jetting camera/jetting as separate service).

5) **Cleanup**
   - Final rinse of splash areas.
   - Remove any debris from ground/roofline.

6) **Proof Pack AFTER + completion**
   - Match before angles.
   - Send completion message + link.

## Quality checks (QA)
- No visible debris remaining.
- Water test indicates flow.
- Downspout outlet verified.
- No debris left on property.

## Common failure modes
- **Mess below gutters** → prevention: containment system + slower work.
- **Skipping downspout verification** → prevention: make outlets part of the checklist.
- **Ladder damage** → prevention: stabilizer + mindful placement.

## Customer communication
- Show what was found (pine straw, shingle grit).
- Recommend maintenance cadence (often 2x/year; more with pines).
- If downspout restriction: explain clearly and offer separate solution.

## Upsells / bundles
- Gutter brightening (separate)
- Roof soft wash / house wash
- Drain jetting (if buried drain lines)
