# Flatwork Surface Cleaning SOP v1 (Operator)

> Superseded by: `04_sops/flatwork/flatwork-surface-cleaning-sop-v2.md`

## Service
Driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, and other hard flat surfaces.

> Operator principle: Flatwork is where pressure washing belongs — but the goal is **consistent cleaning**, not damage. Protect adjacent landscaping and painted surfaces.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Flatwork is evenly cleaned with minimized tiger-striping.
- No damage to joints, coatings, pavers, or adjacent surfaces.
- Runoff managed responsibly.
- Proof Pack complete.

## Safety / PPE
- Eye protection.
- Hearing protection.
- Boots with traction.
- Gloves.
- Avoid overspray on people/pets.

## Pre-checks (arrival)
- Identify substrate: concrete vs pavers vs coated surfaces vs stone.
- Identify risks:
  - joint sand loss (pavers)
  - etching potential
  - existing coatings
  - cracks/spalling
- Identify nearby sensitive zones: garage doors, stucco, painted trim, delicate landscaping.
- Photograph pre-existing damage.

## Tools / equipment
- Pressure washer (appropriate GPM for surface cleaner size).
- Surface cleaner sized to GPM.
- Edge wand/gun for borders.
- Optional pre-treatment applicator (soft wash / downstream as appropriate).
- Recovery tools where needed (silt socks, berms).

## Chemicals (ranges + why) — operator-only
- Pre-treatment may be used to loosen organic growth and reduce dwell/pressure needs.
- Avoid publishing exact mixes publicly.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Setup
1. Pre-wet nearby landscaping.
2. Protect adjacent surfaces from overspray.
3. Establish runoff path.

### 2) Pre-treat (if needed)
1. Apply appropriate pre-treatment to organics/staining.
2. Allow dwell while monitoring.

### 3) Surface clean
1. Use consistent overlap passes.
2. Maintain steady walking speed.
3. Keep the surface cleaner level.

### 4) Edge work
1. Hit borders/edges with wand.
2. Avoid blasting joints.

### 5) Rinse + post-treat (if policy)
1. Rinse thoroughly.
2. Post-treat organics if needed.

### 6) Proof Pack
- Before/after from consistent angles.

## Quality checks (QA)
- Check for striping.
- Check edges and transitions.
- Check overspray on walls/doors.

## Common failure modes
- Tiger striping: inconsistent speed/overlap.
- Etching: too much pressure/too close/too slow.
- Paver joint loss: aggressive edges.

## Customer communication
- Set expectations about old stains vs organic growth.
- Explain safety and runoff handling.

## Upsells / bundles
- House wash
- Roof soft wash
- Paver sealing (where applicable)

## Post-job cleanup
- Final rinse of landscaping.
- Pick up debris.
- Document completion.
