# Safety SOP — Working Outside: Heat, Sun, Hydration (Florida)

## Why this matters
Heat exhaustion and heat stroke are real jobsite risks in Florida. This is a *professionalism* issue, not a toughness contest.

## Risk factors
- High humidity (reduced cooling)
- Direct sun on roofs/concrete
- Dark clothing, PPE heat load
- Caffeine/energy drinks
- Poor sleep

## Early warning signs
- Headache, dizziness
- Nausea
- Muscle cramps
- Confusion/irritability
- Stopping sweating (late/danger)

## The TBSoftWash standard (draft)
- Pre-hydrate before the first job
- Water + electrolytes on every truck
- Scheduled shade breaks
- “Buddy check” on roofs

## Work planning
- Heavy jobs earlier in day
- Roof work: rotate tasks, keep communications constant
- If symptoms appear: stop work, cool down, hydrate, escalate

## Emergency plan
- Heat stroke signs = call 911
- Rapid cooling (shade, cold packs, wet towels)
