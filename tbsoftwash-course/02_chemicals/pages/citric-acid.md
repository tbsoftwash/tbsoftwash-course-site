# Citric Acid

## Chemical
Citric acid — a milder acid used for certain brightening and mineral-related tasks.

## Known as
- citric acid

## Used for
- mild brightening
- some light mineral-related cleanup tasks (substrate-dependent)

## Not used for / contraindications
- Not a replacement for organic growth removal.
