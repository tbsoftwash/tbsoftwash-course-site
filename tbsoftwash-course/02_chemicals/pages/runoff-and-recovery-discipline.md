# Runoff + Recovery Discipline (Operator)

## Category
This is the flatwork cousin of rinse discipline.

## Why it matters
Runoff is where:
- stains spread
- plants get stressed
- customers get angry

## Core rules
- Know where the water is going.
- Don’t push dirty water into landscaping.
- Use berms/silt socks when needed.
- Final rinse the edges and transitions.
