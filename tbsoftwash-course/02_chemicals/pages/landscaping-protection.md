# Landscaping Protection (Operator)

## Category
This is not an “add-on.”

Landscaping protection is part of the job.

## Why it matters
Most of your biggest liability stories involve:
- plant stress/burn
- concentrated runoff
- failure to rinse throughout

## The core rule
**Pre-wet. Rinse throughout. Final rinse.**

Not “rinse at the end.”

## High-risk situations
- delicate ornamentals (hibiscus, annuals)
- concentrated downspout discharge into beds
- windy conditions (drift)
- hot sun (drying risk)
- gutter brightening or specialty stain work

## Standard protection workflow
1) Pre-wet sensitive plants before any application.
2) Stage a dedicated rinse line.
3) During dwell: rinse plants periodically.
4) Control downspouts when needed (redirect/extend/bag).
5) Final rinse: plants + hard surfaces.

## Customer language
- “We protect landscaping as part of the service. We pre-wet and rinse throughout.”

## Proof / documentation
- Photograph sensitive landscaping before starting on high-risk jobs.
- Document protection steps (during photo) in high-stakes work.
