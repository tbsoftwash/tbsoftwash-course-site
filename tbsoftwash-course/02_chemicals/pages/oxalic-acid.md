# Oxalic Acid

## Chemical
Oxalic acid — a specialty acid used for rust staining and certain brightening tasks.

## Known as
- oxalic acid
- rust remover (category)

## Used for
- rust staining (substrate-dependent)
- some wood brightening contexts (process-dependent)

## Not used for / contraindications
- Not a substitute for organic growth removal.
- Not for casual use without test spots.

## Typical dilution / range (operator)
Do not publish recipes publicly.

Operator guidance:
- Always test spot.
- Control dwell and rinse thoroughly.

## Surfactant notes
- Depends on application method and substrate.

## Dwell time
- Controlled dwell; do not allow to dry.

## PPE
- Eye protection
- Gloves
- Long sleeves

## Storage & transport
- Keep sealed; avoid moisture exposure if product form requires.

## Neutralization / runoff
- Rinse thoroughly.
- Control runoff.

## Safe surfaces
- Substrate-specific; test and verify.

## Avoid surfaces
- Any surface where acid damage is likely.

## Common failure modes
- Uneven brightening
- Damage to surrounding finishes

## What to say to the customer (expectations)
- “Rust/hard-water stains can be stubborn. We’ll do a test spot and set expectations before continuing.”
