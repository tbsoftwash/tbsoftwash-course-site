# Hard-Water + Mineral Stains (Operator)

## Category
Mineral staining (irrigation, hard-water deposits) is different from organic growth.

## Used for
- diagnosing when “it didn’t come clean” is actually minerals/etching
- deciding whether to quote specialty restoration work

## The truth
If you promise hard-water removal inside a standard house wash, you will create callbacks.

## Operator guidance
- Always test spot.
- Match the method to the substrate.
- Protect glass and sensitive finishes.
- Short dwell + thorough rinse.

## Customer language
- “Hard-water staining is different from mildew. We can often improve it, but it’s specialty restoration work and we’ll test a small area first.”

## Common failure modes
- treating minerals like organics
- overpromising
