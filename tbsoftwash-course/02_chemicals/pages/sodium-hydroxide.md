# Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic)

## Chemical
Sodium hydroxide — a high-risk caustic used for certain stripping/cleaning tasks.

## Known as
- sodium hydroxide
- caustic

## Used for
- specialty stripping tasks (process-dependent)

## Not used for / contraindications
- Not for routine house washing.
- Not for roofs.
- High risk to skin/eyes and certain substrates.

## Typical dilution / range (operator)
Do not publish recipes publicly.

Operator guidance:
- Treat as specialty work.
- Test spot.
- Strict PPE.

## Surfactant notes
- Depends on application.

## Dwell time
- Controlled dwell; avoid drying.

## PPE
- Sealed eye protection
- Chemical-resistant gloves
- Long sleeves/pants

## Storage & transport
- Store securely and away from incompatible chemicals.

## Neutralization / runoff
- Plan runoff carefully.

## Safe surfaces
- Substrate-specific.

## Avoid surfaces
- Sensitive finishes.

## Common failure modes
- Damage to finishes
- Skin/eye injury

## What to say to the customer (expectations)
- “This is specialty restoration work. We’ll test first and set expectations.”
