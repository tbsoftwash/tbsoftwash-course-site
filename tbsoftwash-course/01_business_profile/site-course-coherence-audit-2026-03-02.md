Goal: ensure the course does not contradict tbsoftwash.com messaging.

## High-confidence contradictions / mismatches

### 1) Public recipe policy
**Website:** Publishes explicit ratios and formulas (roof mixes, house wash strength, rust removal ratios), with some warning language.
- https://tbsoftwash.com/tampa-bay-soft-wash-and-pressure-cleaning-formulas/
- https://tbsoftwash.com/tampa-bay-soft-wash-pressure-cleaning-diy-cleaning-solutions/

**Course:** Multiple places state we do not publish recipes publicly (or “no public recipes”). Examples:
- `03_curriculum/module-2-equipment-chemicals/lesson-01-chemicals-safety-first.md` ("We do not publish reckless recipes.")
- `02_chemicals/chemical-index.md` ("mixing ranges … not reckless recipes")
- Several chemical pages: `02_chemicals/pages/*` ("Do not publish recipes publicly")
- SOPs: roofs/houses/flatwork SOP v2 files include “No public recipes.”

**Impact:** A learner who found formulas on your public website could view the course as inconsistent (“you *do* publish recipes”).

**Recommended alignment (public-safe):**
- Change course language from absolute “we do not publish recipes publicly” → “this course does not teach paint-by-numbers recipes; we teach process + decision logic + safety.”
- Add a note that TBSoftWash has published formulas historically with warnings, but the course prioritizes safety and context.
- Route recipe-level detail into the planned Recipe Vault behind the one-time disclaimer wall.

### 2) Roof pressure-washing stance (good alignment)
**Website:** Strongly states do not pressure wash standard roofs; soft wash instead.
- https://tbsoftwash.com/should-you-pressure-wash-a-roof/

**Course:** Standard is “No pressure washing roofs.”

**Status:** coherent.

### 3) Soft wash vs pressure wash definitions (mostly aligned)
**Website:** Soft wash = low pressure (garden hose range) and chemicals do the work; pressure wash for flatwork; often combo.
- https://tbsoftwash.com/what-is-the-difference-between-pressure-washing-and-soft-washing/

**Course:** Teaches method selection by substrate; roof/house soft wash; flatwork pressure + post-treat.

**Status:** coherent.
