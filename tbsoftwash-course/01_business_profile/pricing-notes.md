# Pricing notes (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/pricing/

## Package deal framing
- “Combine multiple exterior cleaning services and save!”

## Starting-at anchors (single-story 800–1,000 sq ft examples)
- Roof soft wash: **starting at $299**
- House soft wash: **starting at $199**
- Gutter cleaning: **starting at $149**
- Window cleaning: **starting at $149**
- Flatwork pressure cleaning: **starting at $149**
- Fencing/Lanai soft wash: **starting at $149**

## Payment policy
- Accepts: cash, check, PayPal, cards via phone or QuickBooks invoice.
- Payment due after service complete + satisfied (advance possible).

## Estimating variables called out
- Roof pitch
- Water access
- Delicate landscaping
- Urgency / how fast job must be completed

## Licensing/insurance messaging
- Notes: no “license” per se, but business tax receipt (BTR) is required + renewed yearly.
- Provides proof of general liability + commercial auto via documents page.

## Course takeaways
- Build a “minimums + multipliers” pricing framework tied to these variables.
- Teach payment terms + invoice flow + remote payment SOP.
