# How To Repair High Pressure Hose In The Field — Notes

## Source
- https://tbsoftwash.com/how-to-repair-high-pressure-hose-in-the-field/

## What the post is really about
A practical “operator engineering” lesson: reduce downtime and cost by using **reusable repair fittings** instead of replacing hoses or relying on a hydraulic shop.

## Key points
- Reality framing: hoses will burst; plan for it.
- Cost control: avoid buying a crimper setup unless volume justifies it.
- “Don’t replace, repair” mindset + the advantage of **reusable** fittings.
- Workflow:
  - identify burst
  - cut to fresh end
  - install 2-piece repair fitting
  - tighten flush
  - keep fittings for future hoses

## Signature phrases / quotable lines
- “If you own or work for a pressure washer company, one thing is guaranteed, hoses will burst!”
- “Don’t replace, repair!”

## Operator translation (how we teach this safely)
- Treat as a **truck standard**:
  - spare fittings + 2 wrenches
  - a safe cutting tool strategy
  - confirm rated PSI compatibility
- Add safety emphasis: depressurize line, eye protection, safe cutting posture.
