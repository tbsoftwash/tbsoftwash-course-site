# Should You Buy A Soft Wash System Before You Buy A Pressure Washing System? — Notes

## Source
- https://tbsoftwash.com/should-you-buy-a-soft-wash-system-or-a-pressure-washing-system/

## What the post is really about
A startup strategy post: prioritize **high-margin soft-wash services** first, then add pressure washing as an upsell once you’ve sold premium work.

## Key points
- Your stance: unless you already have commercial pressure contracts, don’t lead with a huge pressure-washing capex.
- Marketing ladder:
  - target roofs first, then house washes/fences/lanais
  - add flatwork pressure washing as a natural “while I’m here” add-on
- Upsell psychology:
  - selling a roof/house first makes adding a few hundred in flatwork easy
  - selling driveway-only customers makes big-ticket roof cleaning feel like a hard upsell
- Encourages budget-friendly start:
  - soft wash system first; pressure washer can be rented/subbed initially
  - emphasizes hose cost and repair cost differences
