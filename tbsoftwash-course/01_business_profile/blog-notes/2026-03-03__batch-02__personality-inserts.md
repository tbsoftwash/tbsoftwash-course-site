## Course-ready inserts (original phrasing, inspired by blog voice)

### Insert: Soft wash vs pressure wash (house/roof)
Soft wash is about results with control: low pressure + the right chemistry + dwell + a gentle rinse. Pressure is a tool — but on the wrong surface it’s a damage multiplier.

### Insert: Roof streaks explanation (operator version)
Those black roof streaks aren’t “dirt.” They’re usually living growth that feeds off humidity and shade patterns. Your job is to kill it safely, rinse clean, and leave the roof intact.

### Insert: Benefits beyond “looks”
A professional exterior clean isn’t just curb appeal. It reduces organic buildup that holds moisture, slows down surface breakdown, and makes future maintenance easier.

### Insert: Gutter brightening expectation line
Gutter brightening is restoration, not “a regular wash.” If we can improve it safely we will — but tiger striping and oxidation are their own scope, their own risk, and their own price.

### Insert: Drain jetting positioning line
Drain jetting isn’t a flex. It’s a controlled way to restore flow in a line without guessing. The standard is access, verification, and documentation — not “we blasted it and hoped.”
