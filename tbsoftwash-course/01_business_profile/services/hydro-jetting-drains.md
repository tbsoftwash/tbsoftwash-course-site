# Hydro-Jetting (French & Deck Drains) — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/hydro-jetting-service/

## Positioning
- Drain clearing for pool deck drains + French drains; eco-friendly (water only).

## Use cases listed
- Pool deck drains, French drains, perimeter/trench/blind drains.

## Safety/limitations
- “Should not cause damage when performed by a professional”; can worsen if drains already compromised.
- Notes it is less aggressive than a pro-grade snake.

## Course takeaways
- Great specialty add-on module: inspection, access points, how to avoid blowback/flooding, when to refer to plumber.
- Tie into gutter/downspout drain issues and “standing water” customer complaints.
