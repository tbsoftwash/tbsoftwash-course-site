# Graffiti Removal — TBSoftWash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/graffiti-removal-service/

## Positioning
- Eco-friendly + highly effective; for cities, property managers, homeowners, building owners.

## Guarantee stance (important differentiator)
- **No blanket guarantee**; depends on paint type, dwell time/age, and substrate porosity. Calls out that “guaranteed removal” without details is a red flag.

## Supported substrates (claimed)
- Stucco, brick, concrete, asphalt, vinyl, etc.

## Process (as stated)
1) Prep: pressure wash loose dirt/debris
2) Apply graffiti removal product + light agitation if needed
3) Power wash rinse; hot water pressure washer when applicable
4) Inspection + correct any misses

## Course takeaways
- Teach risk/expectation management script + how to inspect substrate porosity and paint type.
- Include hot-water + chemical selection decision tree.
