# Paver Cleaning & Sealing — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/paver-sealing-service/

## Positioning
- Protects investment from harsh elements; “must-have” for sale prep/curb appeal.

## Products mentioned (course note: vendor/tooling options)
- SealThane: color enhancement, gloss, UV protection, mold/algae resistant, inhibits weeds/ant mines via joint stabilization.
- Paver SealRx: water-based, color enhancement, joint sand binding, UV/stain/water damage protection.
- Also mentions non-film-forming repellents for concrete/aggregate and natural stone (appearance not altered).

## Frequency
- Suggests sealing every **2–3 years** (rule of thumb).

## Process (as stated)
1) Clean pavers (surface cleaner + turbo nozzle; remove debris between cracks)
2) Wet-sand joints
3) Apply sealer (airless sprayer + push broom)
4) Inspection

## Course takeaways
- Build a “paver system” lesson: cleaning quality → joint sand → correct sealer choice → cure/weather constraints.
- Add safety + slip risk + overspray protection.
- Include expectation management around stains (oil) prior to sealing.
