# Gutter Cleaning — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/gutter-cleaning-service/

## Positioning / why
- Prevent water damage; gutters can’t hold weight of debris/water; standing water = mosquitoes.

## Frequency guidance
- Typically **2x/year** (fall + spring); more with pine straw.
- Clear “signs you need cleaning” checklist (overflow, visible debris, standing water, seam leaks, etc.).

## Gutter guards stance
- Still need cleaning/roof maintenance; debris dams on top can push water back into roofline (shingles/sheathing/fascia/soffit/siding damage).

## Process (as stated)
1) Remove loose debris from roof
2) Remove loose debris from gutters
3) Flush gutters with water to clear downspouts/clogs
4) Inspection + correct any misses

## Residential + commercial angles
- Mentions after-hours + “zero mess” for commercial.

## Exclusions / related services
- Gutter brightening (separate)
- Hydro-jetting service for buried downspouts / drain clearing

## Course takeaways
- Teach “guards aren’t maintenance-free” + water-backup failure modes.
- SOP should include roof valley/roofline debris management + cleanup/no-mess standards.
