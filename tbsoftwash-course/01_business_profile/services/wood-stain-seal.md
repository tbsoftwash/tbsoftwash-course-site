# Wood Stain & Seal (Fences/Decks) — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/wood-stain-and-seal-service/

## Positioning
- Fence cleaning + staining + sealing framed as a curb-appeal “must-have”.
- Claims capability for fences, decks, and “raw exterior wood”.
- Product preference stated: **Stain & Seal Experts** (USA-based). Suggests **oil-based**.

## Color options listed
Auburn, Barnwood, Black, Cape Cod Grey, Chocolate, Eucalyptus, Palomino, Sable, Slate Grey, Chestnut, Mahogany, Pecan, Sequoia, Walnut, Honey, Cedar, Redwood, Natural, Clear.

## Frequency / conditions
- Typical re-stain interval: **2–3 years**.
- Best staining conditions: dry weather, **50–90°F**, avoid rainy/humid.

## Process (as stated)
1) Clean fence/deck (saturate work area; protect landscaping; clean)
2) Let fully dry + prep area
3) Spray + brush stain/seal (airless sprayer + brush)
4) Inspection + correct any misses

## Course takeaways
- Separate workflow from pressure washing: drying times + weather windows matter.
- Teach masking/protection, even-coat technique, lap marks, and customer expectations for “natural variation”.
