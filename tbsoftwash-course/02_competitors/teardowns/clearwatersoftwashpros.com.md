# Competitor Teardown — Clearwater Soft Wash Pros

- Website: https://clearwatersoftwashpros.com
- Primary markets: Clearwater/Palm Harbor/Dunedin/Safety Harbor/etc.

## Positioning (what they claim)
- “#1 Pressure Washing & Power Cleaning Services”
- “Best Price Guarantee” + “100% Happiness Guarantee”
- “5-stars service company” (mentions Google/Yelp)
- “Licensed & Insured” and “family owned”

## Offer stack
- Many service pages (roof, house, pavers sealing, solar, windows, commercial)
- Has "Quick Quote" button (ResponsiBid iframe)
- “Schedule free estimate” (Markate)

## Estimate flow (friction audit)
- Strong CTA options:
  - Quick Quote
  - Schedule Free Estimate
  - Phone
- 3-step process marketing (“Get quote → schedule → be wowed”) — common but effective.

## Proof system
- Heavy review emphasis (mentions Google/Yelp)
- Guarantees are prominent; proof packs not explicitly presented in extracted content.

## Content strategy
- Many service area pages (town-by-town) + service pages — strong local SEO footprint.

## Operational signals
- After-hours availability
- “Transparent pricing” and discounts

## What we should steal (Tampa Bay Soft Wash integration)
- Multi-CTA quote flow: phone + form + quick quote.
- Service area page strategy (city pages).

## What we should avoid
- “Best price guarantee” if Tampa Bay Soft Wash is staying top 50–75% pricing.
- Overstuffed claims without matching proof artifacts.

## Course lesson ideas
- Module 9 sales: quote intake options and when to use each.
- Module 10 marketing: city/service page playbook.
