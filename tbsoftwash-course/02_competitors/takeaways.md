# Aggregated competitor takeaways (rolling)

## Patterns observed (first 3 teardowns)

### 1) Soft-wash safety education wins attention
Competitors that explain “why not pressure wash roofs” and frame it as **treat + protect** create instant trust.

Action for TBSoftWash:
- Keep the homeowner explainer as a standard asset (roof + house + gutters).

### 2) Multi-CTA quote flow is common
Phone + form + “quick quote” is everywhere.

Action for TBSoftWash:
- Decide your preferred quote lanes (phone/text vs form vs quick quote) without racing to the bottom on price.

### 3) Guarantees are used heavily (often price guarantees)
Some competitors lead with “best price guarantee” + “happiness guarantee.”

Action for TBSoftWash:
- If top 50–75% pricing is the posture, avoid “best price” messaging; instead lead with proof, process, and protection.

### 4) Service area pages are a real moat
Town-by-town pages plus service pages are a major local SEO play.

Action for TBSoftWash:
- Build a repeatable city/service page template driven by Proof Packs.
