# Module 3 — Roof Soft Wash: Customer Communication (Lesson)

## What you’re doing today
You’re going to learn how to explain roof washing in a way that:
- sets expectations
- reduces anxiety
- prevents disputes
- protects reviews

## Why this matters
Roof cleaning is a premium service. Premium customers expect:
- clear communication
- property protection
- proof

If you communicate well, you get fewer callbacks and better reviews.

## The 20-second roof explanation (say it like this)
- “We don’t pressure wash roofs.”
- “We protect landscaping and manage runoff the whole time.”
- “We treat the growth, let it dwell, rinse gently, and we document before/after.”

## The two lines that prevent 90% of roof callbacks
Say these **before** you start (not after):

1) **“Full results take a few good rains to clear the dead growth. The treatment keeps working after we leave.”**
2) **(If heavy lichen/moss) “You may see some shadowing after the growth clears. That’s the shingle carrying years of growth history — not something we missed.”**

## Step-by-step communication timeline
1) **Before the job (confirm scope)**
   - roof type + access limitations (walkable vs no-walk)
   - what we do / what we don’t do (no high pressure)
   - set the “rains finish the rinse” expectation (the 2 lines above)

2) **Arrival message (set the tone)**
   - remind: no pressure washing
   - plant/runoff protection is part of the job
   - tell them where you’ll be working (ladder/eave/ground)

3) **If we find issues (mid-job)**
   - document and message before you proceed
   - examples:
     - loose tile / mortar cap
     - skylight risk
     - solar panel proximity

4) **Completion message (closeout)**
   - Proof Pack link + thank you
   - reinforce expectations (results develop)
   - invite feedback (fix it fast)
   - review ask timing: 24–72 hours

### The closeout line we use (copy/paste)
> There was a lot of square footage that we cleaned today — if you see **anything at all** that bothers you, call us. We’ll be happy to come back out and fix whatever it is that’s bothering you. I would much rather fix it than let it bother you — seriously, don’t hesitate.

## Decision points
- If slow improvement is likely:
  - say it before you start and document it.

- If the roof is no-walk (limitations):
  - say it before you start
  - document the method and any access limitations in your Proof Pack notes

- If there’s loose tile / mortar caps:
  - photograph it
  - tell the customer before you start

- If a customer is anxious:
  - show one Proof Pack example so they understand the process.

## Common mistakes + fixes
- Over-explaining chemistry
  - Fix: explain process + safety + what to expect.
- Promising a perfect restoration without seeing the roof dry
  - Fix: promise a professional process, document exceptions.
