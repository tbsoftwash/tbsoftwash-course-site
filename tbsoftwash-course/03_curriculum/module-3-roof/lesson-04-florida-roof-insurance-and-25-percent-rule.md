# Module 3 — Roof Soft Wash: Shingle vs. Tile vs. Metal & Florida Insurance Rules (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

In Florida, roof soft washing is rarely a "cosmetic" purchase—it is an **insurance retention and roof-life preservation service**. Underwriting carriers use aerial satellite imagery (Nearmap / EagleView) to flag black *Gloeocapsa magma* streaks as "accelerated granule loss or ponding." When you understand roof substrates and Florida building codes, you become a trusted advisor to homeowners and Realtors.

## 1. Substrate Matrix: Shingle vs. Barrel Tile vs. Coastal Metal

| Roof Substrate | Primary Biological / Environmental Threat | Operator Soft Wash Protocol & Risk Controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Architectural Fiberglass Shingle** | *Gloeocapsa magma* cyanobacteria feeding on limestone filler (`CaCO3`), causing curling & heat retention |Strictly ARMA-compliant low-pressure (`< 60 PSI`) application from ladder/ground; **never** use a pressure washer or walk brittle sun-baked slopes |
| **Concrete & Clay Barrel Tile** | Deep-pore black algae, green moss, and yellow/grey lichen anchored in porous tile valleys | Two-coat dwell cycle (let chemistry dissolve root hyphae rather than scrubbing); walk only on the structural bottom pan overlap when roof access is mandatory |
| **Coastal Metal (`Kynar 500®` PVDF & `Galvalume`)** | Salt-air oxidation, pine tannin staining, and clear-coat chalking along Clearwater/Gulf beaches | Lower chemical strength + high surfactant rinse; test for chalky `TiO2` oxidation first and flush immediately with high-volume fresh water |

## 2. Florida Statutory Positioning (`SB 4-D`, `SB 2-D` & `FBC § 706.1.1`)
- **Satellite Non-Renewal Notices:** Carriers frequently mistake black algae streaks on 8–14 year old roofs for structural wear. Our timestamped **Before/After Proof Pack** gives the homeowner's 4-Point inspector clear visual evidence of intact shingles/tiles after soft washing.
- **Florida Building Code `§ 706.1.1` (`SB 4-D` / `F.S. § 553.844(5)` — The 25% Roof Rule):** Roofs permitted after March 1, 2009 that meet the 2007 FBC are no longer forced into full replacement when a section is repaired, provided the rest of the roof passes inspection. Soft washing removes organic camouflage so inspectors can verify true remaining useful life under `F.S. § 627.7011` (`SB 2-D`).
