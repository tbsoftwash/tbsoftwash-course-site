# Spill + Exposure Response Card v1 (Printable)

Print this. Keep it in the truck.

## If a chemical gets on skin
1) Stop work.
2) Rinse with running water (lots of it).
3) Remove contaminated clothing.
4) Consult SDS first aid section.

## If a chemical gets in eyes
1) Flush immediately (eyewash).
2) Keep flushing (do not “wait and see”).
3) Consult SDS and seek medical attention as needed.

## If fumes are inhaled
1) Move to fresh air.
2) Stop the source.
3) Consult SDS and seek medical attention as needed.

## If there is a spill
1) Stop the source (upright container / shut valve).
2) Contain (prevent reaching storm drains / landscaping).
3) Dilute/rinse per product + SDS guidance.
4) Document what happened (photos + notes).

## Non‑negotiables
- Do not mix chemicals.
- Do not let products dry on surfaces.
- When in doubt: stop, rinse, reassess.
