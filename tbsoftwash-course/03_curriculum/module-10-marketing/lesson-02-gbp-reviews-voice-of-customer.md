# Module 10 — Marketing Engine: GBP + Reviews + Voice of Customer (Lesson)

## What customers actually say (and why it matters)
Your best marketing is the customer’s words.

Use this doc to build your script library and your proof captions:
- `05_sales_marketing/reviews-voice-of-customer.md`

## Review ask timing
We typically ask right at the end of the walkaround.

The simplest version:
> “If you use Google, we would really appreciate a Google review.”

Some customers don’t use Google — that’s fine. If they’re a Facebook person, take the win there.

- ask 24–72 hours after completion (follow-up if needed)

Use:
- `05_sales_marketing/review-request-and-response-sop.md`

## Review responses
Warm. Specific. Short.

We don’t run on “the customer is always right.” But we do run on math.

Ask yourself: is a negative Google review really worth refusing a small fix that takes five minutes — even if it wasn’t in the original scope?

> **Authority Lens:** Reviews aren’t just conversion. They’re a prominence/trust signal. If two companies are equally close and relevant, trust often becomes the tie-breaker.

Mention:
- the service
- one detail
- gratitude
