# Module 7 — Flatwork: Commercial Drive-Thrus, Dumpster Pads & Oil Stain Saponification (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

Residential concrete is 90% organic algae (*Gloeocapsa magma*), which responds to sodium hypochlorite. **Commercial flatwork**—QSR drive-thru lanes, restaurant dumpster pads, loading docks, and HOA parking stalls—is loaded with **animal fats, vegetable oils (triglycerides), and petroleum hydrocarbons**. Bleach alone will not touch heavy grease. To win recurring commercial route contracts, you must master **alkaline saponification** and **stormwater recovery**.

## 1. Chemistry Science: Why Caustic Soda (`NaOH`) Beats Bleach on Grease
- **Saponification (`Triglycerides + NaOH → Soap + Glycerol`):** [Sodium Hydroxide (Caustic Soda Beads)][sodium_hydroxide] reacts with animal/vegetable fats and oxidized motor oil, converting hydrophobic grease into water-soluble soap that your surface cleaner and hot/high-flow rinse can lift out of the concrete pores.
- **Trident Protects Commercial Degreasers:** For heavy tire marks, hydraulic fluid, and sensitive adjacent substrates, we pair **Trident Protects Break Wall** and **Sea Wall** commercial degreasers with controlled dwell times (`10–15 minutes`, never allowing chemical to dry).
- **Never Mix Caustics with Acids:** Keep [Sodium Hydroxide][sodium_hydroxide] completely isolated from [Oxalic Acid][oxalic_acid] and [Acid Magic][acid_magic] on the truck.

## 2. Nozzle Sizing & NPDES Wastewater Containment
- **Surface Cleaner MEG Orifice Matching:** Match your 2-bar or 4-bar surface cleaner nozzles to your machine's GPM (e.g., two `25025` tips for a 5 GPM machine, or two `25040` tips for an 8 GPM machine) so impact pressure stays at `2,500–3,000 PSI` without etching cream off commercial concrete.
- **NPDES / Municipal Stormwater Compliance:** Under Pinellas County Code Chapter 58 Article VI and City of Tampa stormwater rules, emulsified grease and dumpster leachate **cannot** flow into street storm grates. Block storm inlets, divert wash water to permeable swales where permitted, or evacuate pooled grease water using a [Suttner ST-36 Pit Boss Sludge Sucker][suttner_st36_sludge_sucker].
