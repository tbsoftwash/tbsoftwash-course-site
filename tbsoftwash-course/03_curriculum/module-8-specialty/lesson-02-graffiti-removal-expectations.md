# Module 8 — Specialty: Commercial Graffiti Removal & Barrier Protection (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

When spray-paint vandalism hits a commercial shopping plaza, HOA perimeter wall, or municipal sign, the clock is ticking: **the first 24–48 hours** determine whether aerosol solvents cure deep inside porous masonry or lift cleanly without ghosting. Fast-response graffiti abatement is a high-margin entry point into commercial property management accounts.

## 1. Substrate Risk Matrix (Never Blast Blindly)
1. **Unpainted Porous Brick, CMU Block & Concrete:** Absorbs aerosol pigments deep into capillaries. Requires a clinging gel/paste stripper dwell (`10–20 minutes`) followed by controlled medium-pressure fan rinsing (`1,200–1,800 PSI`) from bottom to top—never etch brick mortar with a `0°` or turbo tip.
2. **Painted Stucco & Synthetic EIFS:** High pressure will blow the stucco finish coat off EPS foam board, and aggressive solvents will melt the underlying acrylic paint. Always run a **60-second inconspicuous test spot** and set customer expectations that heavily oxidized paint may show a clean spot ("reverse halo") requiring touch-up paint.
3. **Metal & Polycarbonate Commercial Signage / Utility Boxes:** Never use methylene chloride or harsh ketones that cloud polycarbonate plastic. Use citrus/ester-based removers and lint-free [Arkwright Surgical Huck Towels][huck_towels].
4. **Pro Upsell — Sacrificial / Permanent Anti-Graffiti Barrier:** On unpainted brick, concrete walls, or paver monuments, upsell a breathable **Trident Protects** barrier sealer so future tags wash off without deep pore penetration.
