# Module 8 — Specialty: Electrostatic Gutter Brightening (Tiger Stripe Removal) (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

One of the fastest ways to lose money—or ruin a customer's expectations—on a standard house wash is not explaining the difference between **organic mildew** and **electrostatic gutter oxidation ("tiger stripes")**. Standard house-wash bleach (`1%–1.5% SH`) kills green/black algae on gutters, but it **will not remove** vertical black electrostatic runoff lines bonded to the baked-on aluminum enamel. That requires **Gutter Brightening**—sold as a dedicated specialty upgrade.

## 1. The Science of "Tiger Stripes" (Why Bleach Doesn't Touch Them)
- Vertical black streaks on white aluminum K-style gutters are composed of **asphalt shingle petroleum leachates, atmospheric carbon soot, and oxidized aluminum particles** electrostatically bonded to the factory polyester/acrylic paint film.
- Breaking that electrostatic bond requires a **butyl/alkaline surfactant degreaser** ([LA's Totally Awesome Concentrated Degreaser][las_totally_awesome_1gal] or dedicated gutter brightener) applied with soft mechanical brush agitation via an [Ettore Wrap-Around Flow Brush][ettore_extend_a_flo_brush] on an [Ettore 16-Foot Extension Pole][ettore_extension_pole] or [Gardiner Water-Fed Pole][water_fed_pole_gardiner].

## 2. Critical Failure Mode: Stripping Baked Enamel Paint
- **Never Let Brightener Dry on Hot Florida Aluminum:** If butyl degreaser flashes dry on sun-baked gutters (`90°F+`), or is mixed too hot, it will strip the white factory paint down to bare gray aluminum—or drip down and burn streaks into dark exterior house paint below.
- **Work in 10-Foot Sections:** Pre-wet the gutter face and the siding/plants below, scrub a `10-foot` section with diluted brightener on your soft brush, and immediately flush with high-volume fresh water ([General Pump RB35 Wash Down Gun][wash_down_gun_low_pressure]) before moving to the next section.
