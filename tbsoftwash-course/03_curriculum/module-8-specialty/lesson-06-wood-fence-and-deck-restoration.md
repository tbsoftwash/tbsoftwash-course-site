# Module 8 — Specialty: Two-Step Wood Fence & Deck Restoration (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

Homeowners think a gray, splintered pressure-treated pine or cedar fence needs to be blasted with 3,000 PSI. If you hit soft Florida wood with high pressure—or nuked it with roof-strength bleach—you will **furr the wood grain** (destroy the lignin fibers) and leave wand marks that show through every stain coat. True wood restoration is a **two-step pH-balanced chemical process** using gentle rinse pressure (`500–800 PSI`).

## 1. The Two-Step Chemistry System (Clean + Brighten)

| Step | Chemical Agent | What It Does to the Wood | Field Discipline |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Step 1: Oxygen Cleaning (Alkaline)** | **[Sodium Percarbonate (Oxygen Bleach)][sodium_percarbonate]** (or buffered percarbonate/metasilicate wash; [Sodium Hydroxide][sodium_hydroxide] only when stripping old failed oil stain) | Breaks down dead gray UV-oxidized wood cells, mold spores, and dirt without dissolving structural lignin fibers like raw bleach does | Dissolve in warm water, apply bottom-to-top to prevent streaking, allow `10–15 min` wet dwell, then rinse with a wide `40°` white fan tip at `500–800 PSI` |
| **Step 2: pH Neutralization & Brightening (Acidic)** | **[Oxalic Acid Crystals][oxalic_acid]** (or Citric Acid) | Neutralizes the high-pH alkaline cleaner, closes the wood pores, pulls out iron/tannin dark spots (nail bleed), and restores the warm golden honey tone | Apply evenly right after rinsing Step 1 while wood is still wet; dwell `5–10 min`, then give a light final fresh-water rinse |

## 2. Setting Customer Expectations (Before You Start)
- **Wand Marks from Prior DIY Washing:** If the homeowner or a previous contractor already scarred the fence with a `15°` tip at 3,000 PSI, chemical restoration will clean and brighten the wood, but only mechanical sanding removes physical gouges. Document pre-existing wand scars in your **Pre-Condition Proof Pack**.
- **Stain Readiness (`< 12–15%` Moisture):** Wood must dry for `48–72 hours` in Florida sun before applying penetrating oil or sealer.
