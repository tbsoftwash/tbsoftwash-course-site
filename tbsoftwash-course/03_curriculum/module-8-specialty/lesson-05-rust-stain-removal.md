# Module 8 — Specialty: Well-Water Irrigation Rust & Fertilizer Stain Removal SOP (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

In Tampa Bay and coastal Florida, shallow-aquifer well water is loaded with dissolved ferrous iron (`Fe2+`). When sprinkler heads mist white stucco, concrete driveways, vinyl fences, or pool cages, that iron oxidizes on contact with air into insoluble orange/red **ferric hydroxide (`Fe(OH)3`)**. Add orange ferrous-sulfate lawn fertilizer granules tracked onto wet concrete, and you have one of the highest-margin specialty upsells in our business—provided you respect **acid chelation chemistry** and **neutralization discipline**.

## 1. Required PPE (Non-Negotiable)
Before opening any acid container or mixing dry crystals, equip full sealed eye, skin, and respiratory protection:
- **[3M Splash-Proof Safety Goggles][safety_goggles]** — must seal against your face (never rely on open side-glasses when spraying overhead or into wind).
- **[LANON Chemical-Resistant Nitrile Gloves][nitrile_gloves]** — gauntlet cuff protection.
- **[3M Half-Face Respirator][respirator_half_face]** equipped with **[3M Organic Vapor / Acid Gas Cartridges][vapor_cartridge]**.

## 2. Chelation Chemistry: Matching the Acid to the Stain
Never pump acid through your 12V soft wash manifold or pressure washer injector—always apply using a dedicated **[Chapin Viton-Sealed 1-Gallon Pump Sprayer][chapin_pump_sprayer]** or **[Chemical Guys 32oz Acid-Resistant Trigger Sprayer][chemical_guys_acid_sprayer]**:
1. **[Oxalic Acid 99% Crystals][oxalic_acid] (Organic Dicarboxylic Acid Chelator):** Our go-to for well-water irrigation rust on concrete, stucco, and vinyl siding. Oxalic acid converts insoluble ferric iron (`Fe3+`) back into water-soluble ferrous oxalate complex without etching concrete cream the way raw muriatic acid does. Dissolve in warm water for faster activation.
2. **[Acid Magic][acid_magic] (Buffered Hydrochloric/Muriatic Alternative):** Emits 90% fewer fumes than raw HCl; reserved for heavy mineral scale, efflorescence crust, or deep iron deposits on unpainted masonry. *Golden Rule: Always add acid to water, never water to acid.*
3. **[Bar Keepers Friend Powdered Cleanser][bar_keepers_friend]:** Ideal for localized hard-water/rust detailing on glass doors, stainless fixtures, and porcelain with [Arkwright Surgical Huck Towels][huck_towels].

## 3. Application, Dwell & Sodium Bicarbonate Neutralization
1. **Pre-Wet Adjoining Surfaces & Plants:** Saturate surrounding turf, plants, and bordering concrete so acid cannot flash-dry or wick deep into dry edges (which creates a bright "clean halo" on old concrete).
2. **Controlled Wet Dwell (`5–10 Minutes`):** Apply evenly over the full concrete panel (joint-to-joint to prevent spot halos). Never let the solution dry on the surface.
3. **Neutralize Before Final Flush:** Rinsing alone leaves acidic runoff in flower beds. Mist treated areas and runoff edges with **[Arm & Hammer Baking Soda][baking_soda]** solution from a dedicated **[Chemical-Resistant Spray Bottle][spray_bottle]**, verify `pH >= 7.0` using **[0-14 pH Test Strips][ph_test_strips]**, and flush thoroughly with fresh water.

> **⚠️ CRITICAL CHEMICAL SAFETY WARNING:** Never apply acid over un-rinsed sodium hypochlorite (bleach), and never store acids next to bleach tanks. Mixing acid + bleach releases lethal **chlorine gas (`Cl2`)**.
