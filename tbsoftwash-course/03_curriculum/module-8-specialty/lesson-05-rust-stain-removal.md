# Module 8 — Specialty: Rust Stain Removal SOP (Lesson)

Rust removal is a high-value upsell, but it introduces one of the highest safety risks in our business: **Acid.** 

Whether you are dealing with irrigation rust on a sidewalk or battery acid stains on a driveway, this SOP dictates exactly how to treat the stain, protect the surrounding property, and most importantly, protect yourself.

## Required PPE (Non-Negotiable)
Before opening any acid container, you must have the following equipped. Acid fumes can permanently damage your lungs and eyes.
- **[3M Splash-Proof Safety Goggles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00467C7AE?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** (Do not use standard safety glasses; they must seal against your face).
- **[Chemical-Resistant Nitrile Gloves](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07ZRM6SZN?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** (Gauntlet style preferred).
- **[3M Half-Face Respirator](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B007JZ1N00?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** equipped with **[Organic Vapor/Acid Gas Cartridges](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00AEFCKKY?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)**.

## The Chemistry: What We Use

We utilize two primary products depending on the severity of the rust and the surface. Do not put acid in your soft wash system—apply it using a dedicated **[Viton-sealed Chemical Pump Sprayer](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00295QD96?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)**.

### 1. Oxalic Acid (Dry Form)
**[Oxalic Acid Crystals](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0147JPQAI?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** are cheap, stable, and highly effective for standard rust stains. 
* **Dilution:** Mix 1 to 2 cups of dry oxalic acid with 1 gallon of warm water. 
* **Paste:** For vertical or stubborn spots, mix a 3:1 ratio (powder to water) to create a paste that won't run.

### 2. Acid Magic (Liquid)
**[Acid Magic](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000KKQ8LA?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** is a buffered muriatic acid alternative. It emits 90% fewer fumes and won't immediately burn intact skin, but it remains a highly aggressive acid.
* **Dilution:** Start with a 1:4 ratio (1 part Acid Magic to 4 parts water). Remember the golden rule: *Always add acid to water, never water to acid.*

## Application & Dwell Time
1. **Pre-wet everything.** Saturate all surrounding vegetation, grass, and adjoining concrete. Acid will flash-dry on a dry surface and cause permanent etching.
2. Evenly apply your acid solution using your pump sprayer.
3. Allow it to dwell for **5 to 10 minutes**. 
4. **Never let it dry.** Keep it misted with water or reapply the solution if needed. 
5. Assess the stain. If it hasn't lifted, increase the chemical ratio and reapply.

## Rinse and Neutralize
Rinsing is not enough. You must neutralize the acid to stop the chemical reaction.

Keep a **[Chemical-Resistant Spray Bottle](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B074PTQZRP?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** filled with acid neutralizer on your truck at all times. 
* **Neutralizer Recipe:** Mix ½ cup of **[Baking Soda](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000N3BBW0?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** with 1 quart of water.

If acid splashes on grass, metal, or unintended surfaces, immediately spray it with neutralizer and flush it heavily with a garden hose. 

## Clean Up & Disposal
To discard leftover acid solution, pour baking soda into the bucket until it stops bubbling. 
You can use **[0-14 pH Test Strips](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B00X9D4JS4?tag=tbsoftwash06-20)** to verify the pH is above 7.0. Once neutralized into a harmless salt, dispose of it safely away from drains or pets.

> **⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING:** Never mix acid with bleach (sodium hypochlorite). Doing so creates toxic chlorine gas which can be fatal.
