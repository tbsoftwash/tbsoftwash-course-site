# Module 8 — Specialty: Paver Sealing & Restoration System (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

Paver sealing is one of the highest-ticket services in Florida exterior cleaning—and the #1 service where amateur contractors fail because they trap moisture, use hardware-store acrylic sealers that turn milky white, or sweep generic play sand into joints. At Tampa Bay Soft Wash, we exclusively run **Trident Protects** professional restoration chemistry and **ASTM C-144** angular washed silica joint sand.

## 1. Why Paver Jobs Turn White & Hazy (Diagnosis First)
Before quoting a paver job, diagnose which of the 3 failure modes is present:
1. **Failed Hardware-Store Topical Sealer (Blushing):** Non-breathable solvent/acrylic coats trap ground vapor from Florida's high water table, turning milky white and peeling. *Fix:* Chemical stripping with **Trident Break Wall** or **Sea Wall** before resealing.
2. **Efflorescence (`CaO → Ca(OH)2 → CaCO3`):** Calcium oxide in concrete pavers reacts with pore water and migrates to the surface as calcium hydroxide, which reacts with atmospheric `CO2` to form insoluble white calcium carbonate crust. **New Paver Rule:** Never seal brand-new concrete pavers before their **30–90 day efflorescence cure window** completes, and always chemically open/clean pores with **Trident Jetty** efflorescence cleaner first.
3. **Polymeric Sand Haze:** Amateur installers wash polymer binders out of the joints onto the paver face where it cures like cloudy glue. We stabilize joints strictly with wet-washed **ASTM C-144** angular silica sand that locks into our two-part urethane floods without surface haze.
