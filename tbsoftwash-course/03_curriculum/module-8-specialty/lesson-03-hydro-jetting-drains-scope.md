# Module 8 — Specialty: Hydro-Jetting Pool Deck & French Drains (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

In Florida, every heavy summer thunderstorm or hurricane feeder band exposes clogged pool-deck channel drains (Deco-Drain), buried downspout tie-ins, and corrugated French drains choked with oak leaves, sand, and roof granules. **Hydro-jetting** uses your existing 4–8 GPM pressure washer + a flexible sewer-jetter hose and rear-thruster nozzle to pull itself through underground PVC/corrugated pipe, pulverizing blockages and flushing silt out to the pop-up emitter.

## 1. Jetter Nozzle Physics: How It Pulls Itself Around 90° Elbows
- **1 Forward Punch Jet (`0°`):** Cuts through compacted sand plugs, root hairs, and sludge dams directly ahead of the nozzle.
- **3 to 6 Rear Thruster Jets (`30°–45°` rearward angle):** Propel the [WEST BAY 100ft 5800 PSI Sewer Jetter Hose][west_bay_sewer_jetter_100ft] forward deep into the pipe while scouring the interior pipe walls as you slowly retrieve the line.
- **Rotating vs. Button-Nose Tips:** Use the button-nose tip from your [10-Piece Stainless Sewer Jetter Nozzle Kit][sewer_jetter_nozzle_10pc] to clear initial sand blockages, then follow with the spinning rotating nozzle to polish grease/algae slime off the pipe walls.

VIDEO: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Sy1Rg09SiNc | Hydro-jetting example: deck drain unclogging (short). Focus: access points + before/after flow verification.

## 2. Scope Boundaries & Safety (What Hydro-Jetting Cannot Fix)
- **Collapsed Corrugated Pipe or Crushed Bellies:** If a tree root or driveway settling has crushed thin black corrugated pipe underground, hydro-jetting will hit a hard stop or return raw clay/mud. Measure the exact footage mark on your jetter hose where the obstruction sits, mark the lawn above ground, and document it in your **Proof Pack** so the homeowner knows where excavation is needed.
- **Always Control the Line with a Ball Valve:** Mount an [MTM Hydro DN10 Ball Valve][pressure_washer_ball_valve] right before the jetter hose connection—**never** pull a live high-pressure jetter nozzle out of the pipe opening while under pressure! Mark the last `3 feet` of your jetter hose with bright electrical tape as a visual stop warning.
