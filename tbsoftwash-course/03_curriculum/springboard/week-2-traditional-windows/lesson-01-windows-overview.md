# Week 2 — Traditional Windows: Detail + Customer Experience (Lesson)

## Why windows matter
Window cleaning is where customers decide if you’re “a guy with a wand” or a real company.

It’s not just glass.

It’s:
- respect for the home
- no drips inside
- clean edges
- clean closeout

## What you sell (scope clarity)
- inside/out (or exterior only)
- screens (add-on)
- tracks (add-on)
- hard water removal (separate specialty)

## Success looks like
- streak-free glass (sun angle check)
- no interior drips
- floors protected
- Proof Pack (privacy-safe)
