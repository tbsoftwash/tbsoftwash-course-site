# Module 5 — Windows: Method Selection + Safety (Lesson)

## The standard
Windows are reputation work.

A roof job can look great from the street.

A bad window job looks bad from the kitchen.

## Non-negotiables
- No high-pressure blasting on glass, frames, seals, or screen enclosures.
- Diagnose first: soil type + substrate + risk.
- Protect landscaping and manage runoff.

## Method ladder (simple)
Choose the least aggressive method that gets a professional result.

1) **Dry work** (brush off heavy debris, remove cobwebs)
2) **Traditional** (scrub + squeegee)
3) **Water-fed pole (pure water)** (rinse-free when done right)
4) **Detailing** (tracks/screens, add-on)

## What actually causes “bad windows”
- Hard water spotting
- Oxidation transfer (paint/frames)
- Failing seals / weep holes issues
- Soap residue / towel lint
- Dirty screens rinsed onto clean glass

## Safety notes (public-safe)
- Ladder safety is not optional.
- Scrapers are high-risk: use only when appropriate and with fresh blades.
- Never assume “glass is glass.” Coatings and damage matter.
