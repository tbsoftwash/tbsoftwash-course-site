# Module 5 — Windows: Traditional Workflow + Quality Control (Lesson)

## Why traditional still matters
Traditional is the most controllable method.

If you can’t make traditional perfect, WFP won’t save you.

## The workflow (high level)
1) Prep (protect plants, stage towels, check screens)
2) Scrub (even coverage)
3) Squeegee (consistent pulls)
4) Detail edges (no runs)
5) Final check from multiple angles

## Quality control (what the customer notices)
- Edge lines (top corners)
- Sills/drips
- Reflections in bright sunlight
- Screen drip-back

## Common failure modes
- Old rubber
- Dirty towels
- Over-soaping
- Rushing corners
