# Module 6 (Advanced) — Gutters: Brightening vs Cleaning (Lesson)

This module is where you stop treating gutters like a simple add-on and start treating brightening like what it is: **restoration work**.

If you price it like cleaning, you’ll hate your life. If you run it like restoration (risk control + expectations + proof), it becomes a high-margin skill that separates you from the pack.

## The rule
VIDEO: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mOjbUvlr8s8 | Gutter brightening example (short). Watch for: dwell, agitation, rinse, and expectation setting.

Gutter cleaning ≠ gutter brightening.

Brightening is restoration work.

Different chemistry. Different risk. Different price.

## What to sell
- Cleaning: debris removal + downspout verification
- Brightening: tiger strip / oxidation restoration

## Scope Boundary Script (Copy/Paste for Estimates)
- **Gutter Cleaning (Interior Trough & Downspouts):** Clearing leaves, shingle granules, and roof sludge from inside the gutter trough and verifying downspout flow to grade.
- **Gutter Brightening (Exterior Tiger-Stripe Restoration):** Removing vertical black electrostatic oxidation streaks from the outside face of white aluminum gutters using a dedicated butyl surfactant and soft flow-brush agitation. *(See `/course/core/8/gutter-brightening-separate-service` for the complete chemical execution SOP).*
