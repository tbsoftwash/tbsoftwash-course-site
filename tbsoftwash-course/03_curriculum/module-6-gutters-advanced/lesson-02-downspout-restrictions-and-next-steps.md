## The reality
Many “gutter problems” are actually:
- buried drain tie-ins
- clogged elbows
- collapsed lines

## What we do on a standard gutter job
- verify discharge at the outlet
- document restrictions

## What we do next (separate service)
- recommend jetting/camera when indicated

## Communication script
