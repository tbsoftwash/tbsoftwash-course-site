# Module 2 — Equipment & Chemicals: Downstream Injector vs. X-Jet vs. 12V Delivery Systems (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

Every exterior surface requires a specific on-surface chemical strength—and your **delivery method** dictates the maximum concentration that can physically reach the wall or roof. Knowing when to grab a **fixed high-draw downstream injector**, an **X-Jet M5 external nozzle**, or a **dedicated 12V batch/proportioner pump** keeps you from under-treating heavy algae or over-treating delicate paint.

## 1. The 3 Chemical Delivery Lanes

| Delivery System | Typical Draw Ratio | Best Field Application | Primary Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Downstream Injector** ([General Pump 100776][gen_pump_injector_100776]) | `~10:1` to `15:1` (at 4–8 GPM) | Standard stucco/vinyl house washes, pool cages, concrete post-treatment | Cannot reach roof-grade concentrations; long hose runs (`200+ ft`) reduce venturi draw |
| **External Nozzle Injector** ([X-Jet M5][x_jet] / [X-Jet DS Twist][x_jet_twist]) | `~4:1` to `15:1` (via proportioner tips) | Heavy stucco mildew, commercial dumpster pads, 1-story shingle roofs, carrying a 5-gal bucket | Requires carrying or carting a chemical pail near the work zone |
| **Dedicated 12V Soft Wash System** ([Delavan 5850][12v_diaphragm_pump]) | `1:1` to `10:1` (via batch tank or manifold) | Barrel tile & shingle roofs, heavy *Gloeocapsa magma*, brick paver stripping prep | Higher chemical consumption; requires strict plant pre-wetting & neutralization discipline |

## 2. Florida Water Restrictions & NPDES Stormwater Compliance
When operating in Pinellas, Hillsborough, and Pasco Counties:
- **SWFWMD Water Use Compliance:** Licensed pressure washing contractors operating high-efficiency GPM equipment are exempt from residential lawn-watering day restrictions when performing property maintenance, provided shut-off ball valves ([MTM Hydro DN10 Ball Valve][pressure_washer_ball_valve]) and low-volume rinse discipline are used.
- **Pinellas County Code Chapter 58 Article VI (NPDES Stormwater):** Never discharge active chlorine or unrecovered caustic grease sludge into municipal storm drains leading to Tampa Bay estuaries. Keep neutralizer and [Suttner ST-36 Pit Boss Sludge Sucker][suttner_st36_sludge_sucker] containment gear on the truck.
