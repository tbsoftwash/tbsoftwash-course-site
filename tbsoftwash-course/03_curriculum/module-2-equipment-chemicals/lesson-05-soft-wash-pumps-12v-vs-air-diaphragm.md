# Module 2 — Equipment & Chemicals: 12V Diaphragm vs. Air Diaphragm Soft Wash Pumps (Lesson)

Hey Operator,

Choosing your soft wash pump architecture determines your horizontal throw, vertical reach, daily square-footage capacity, and mid-job troubleshooting speed. In this lesson, we break down **12V electric diaphragm pumps** versus **pneumatic air diaphragm (AODD) systems**, plus how to wire and plumb a reliable manifold that survives daily sodium hypochlorite (SH) service.

## 1. Architecture Comparison: 12V Diaphragm vs. Air Diaphragm (All-Flo / Comet)

| Engineering Metric | 12V Diaphragm Rig (`5.0–7.0 GPM`) | Pneumatic Air Diaphragm (`AODD 1/2"`) |
|---|---|---|
| **Flow Rate & Pressure** | `5.0–7.0 GPM` @ `60–100 PSI` | `9.0–12.0 GPM` @ `100 PSI` (requires air compressor) |
| **Vertical Reach** | `25–35 ft` (1–2 story residential roofs & stucco) | `45–60 ft` (3–4 story commercial/COA envelopes & steep tile) |
| **Power Source** | Deep-cycle Group 29/34 marine battery + charger | Gas air compressor (`15+ CFM @ 90 PSI`) |
| **Field Swap Time** | `10 minutes` using quick-connect quad-port fittings | `30–45 minutes` (manifold rebuild or compressor service) |
| **Best Operator Fit** | Single-truck residential & light commercial routes | Multi-story HOA/COA & large barrel-tile roof crews |

## 2. Plumbing & Electrical Non-Negotiables (Why Pumps Die Early)
Most 12V pump failures happen because of **suction starvation**, **voltage drop**, or **unflushed Viton check valves**:
1. **Suction Plumbing (`3/4"` Non-Collapsible):** Never starve a 5–7 GPM pump with thin garden hose on the intake side. Run [HydroMaxx 3/4" Reinforced PVC Suction Hose][hydromaxx_suction_hose_3_4] clamped with [WINL Stainless Steel Worm-Gear Clamps][winl_stainless_hose_clamps].
2. **Proportioner / Blend Manifold Control:** Use [Spears 3/4" Female NPT PVC Gate Valves (Viton O-Ring)][spears_pvc_gate_valve_viton] for smooth, repeatable metering adjustments instead of cheap hardware-store ball valves that stick under SH crystallization.
3. **Electrical Protection:** Wire your [Delavan FB3 7.0 GPM 12V Pump][delavan_fb3_7gpm_pump] or [Delavan 5850 5.0 GPM 12V Pump][12v_diaphragm_pump] through a [10AWG SAE Fuse Harness][dstfuy_10awg_sae_fuse_harness] and [Yak-Power Waterproof Inline Switch][yak_power_12v_inline_switch] housed inside a [Marine Battery Box][marine_battery] powered by a [Banshee Group 34 Deep-Cycle Marine Battery][banshee_group34_marine_battery].
4. **End-of-Day Fresh Water Flush:** Always draw `2–3 gallons` of fresh water + surfactant neutralizer through the pump manifold at the end of every shift so sodium hypochlorite crystals never dry inside the Santoprene diaphragm or Viton valves.
