# Module 4 — House Wash: Diagnose Before You Spray (Lesson)

## The biggest money-loser
Treating oxidation like mildew.

It creates:
- disappointed customers
- callbacks
- “it didn’t work” reviews

## The quick diagnosis
A) Organic growth → soft wash workflow.

B) Oxidation (chalky) → expectation setting + separate restoration if offered.

C) Rust/hard-water/irrigation → specialty treatment or exclude.
