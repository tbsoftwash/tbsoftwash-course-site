# Module 4 — House Wash: Bundles That Sell (Lesson)

## What you’re doing today
You’re going to learn how to sell a house wash like a pro: as an **exterior refresh**, not a pile of line items.

## Why this matters
Bundles increase ticket size without feeling pushy — *if* you can show proof and set expectations.

## Tools & materials (minimum viable)
- Proof Pack photos (before/after)
- A simple bundle menu (3 options)

## The easy bundles (start here)
- House wash + windows
- House wash + gutters
- House wash + flatwork

## Step-by-step: how to present bundles (foreman script)
1) Start with the result: “Full exterior refresh.”
2) Show 1–2 before/after examples (Proof Pack).
3) Offer 3 options:
   - Good: House wash
   - Better: House wash + one add-on
   - Best: House wash + multiple add-ons
4) Set one expectation up front (stains/oxidation/hard-water).
5) Confirm scope in one sentence.

## Decision points
- If the customer is price-sensitive:
  - keep the bundle, adjust scope (not quality).

- If there’s oxidation risk:
  - do not promise restoration; document exceptions.

## Common mistakes + fixes
- Selling “pressure washing” as the product
  - Fix: sell the outcome + protection + consistency.
- Bundling without proof
  - Fix: Proof Pack first, bundles second.

## What good looks like
- Customer can repeat back what’s included.
- You have proof to back up the upsell.

## How to prove it (Proof Pack)
- Before/after of the primary service
- 1 before/after of the add-on you’re recommending
