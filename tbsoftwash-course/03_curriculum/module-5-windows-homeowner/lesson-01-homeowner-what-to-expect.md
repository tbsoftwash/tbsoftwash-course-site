# Windows (Homeowner Lane): What to Expect

## The quick truth
Great window cleaning is about:
- safety
- protection
- technique
- expectations

## What’s usually included (clarify when booking)
- exterior glass cleaning
- wiping edges

Optional add-ons (often separate):
- screen cleaning
- track detailing
- hard water stain treatment

## What window cleaning can’t always fix
- etched glass
- failed seals (fogging between panes)
- permanent hard water staining (restoration may be separate)

## Why we ask questions first
Different windows have different risks:
- oxidation transfer from frames
- coatings
- hard water spotting

## Safety and property protection
We protect:
- landscaping
- screens
- interior floors (if inside work)

## Next step
If you want a premium result, choose the right scope.

- Glass only
- Glass + screens
- Full window package (glass + screens + tracks)
