# 02 — Buyer Intent Rank (Leads)

**Purpose:** rank keywords by **likelihood to convert into calls/quotes**.

## What counts as intent
- Modifiers: **near me**, city names, **company**, **service**, **contractor**
- Transactional: **quote**, **estimate**, **pricing**, **cost**
