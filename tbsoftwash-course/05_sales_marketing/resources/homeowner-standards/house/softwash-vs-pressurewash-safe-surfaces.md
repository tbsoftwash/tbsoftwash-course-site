# Soft Wash vs Pressure Wash: What’s Safe Where? (Homeowner Standards Guide)

## The simple rule
Use the **least aggressive method** that safely gets the result.

## Typically soft-wash (low pressure)
- roofs
- painted siding
- stucco
- screen enclosures

## Typically pressure-wash (controlled)
- concrete flatwork
- some pavers (case-by-case)

## Why this matters
Wrong method can cause:
- paint damage
- water intrusion
- streaking
- etched surfaces
