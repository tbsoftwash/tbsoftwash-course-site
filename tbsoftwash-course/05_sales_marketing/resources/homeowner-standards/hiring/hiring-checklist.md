# Hiring Checklist (Homeowner Standards Guide)

Use this to avoid cheap work that turns into expensive damage.

## Questions to ask
- Are you insured? (Ask for proof.)
- What method will you use on *my* surfaces? (Roof vs siding vs flatwork.)
- How do you protect landscaping and manage runoff?
- How do you document pre-existing damage?
- What are the limitations (oxidation, hard water, etching, seal failure)?

## Red flags
- “We pressure wash roofs.”
- No plan for plants/runoff.
- No written scope or expectations.
- No before/after documentation.
