# Web Authority (Local Service Business) — Notes + Sources

## What “web authority” is
“Web authority” isn’t a single Google metric. It’s shorthand for **how strongly search engines trust and rank your site/brand** relative to competitors.

SEO tools approximate this with their own scores (e.g., Moz **Domain Authority**, Ahrefs **Domain Rating**, Semrush **Authority Score**). These are **third‑party proxies**, not official Google scores.

What’s real underneath the buzzword:

- **Links as endorsements:** quality + relevance of sites that link to you (and how they link).
- **Brand trust signals:** brand searches, reviews, mentions, consistent business info.
- **Topical strength:** you consistently cover the topic (not one thin page).
- **Entity understanding:** Google can confidently identify your business as a real local entity (GBP, citations, consistent NAP).
- **User satisfaction:** pages load fast, match intent, reduce pogo‑sticking.

Think of authority as: **“Do we believe you’re the best answer?”** not “did you stuff keywords.”

## Is authority important for a local service business?
Yes—**with nuance**.

Local results are driven heavily by:

- **Relevance** (does your listing/page match the service intent?)
- **Distance** (proximity)
- **Prominence** (reviews, mentions, links, brand strength)

Authority matters because it increases **prominence/trust**, especially when:

- you’re in a dense competitive market (Tampa Bay = yes),
- you want to rank across multiple nearby cities (beyond “closest”),
- you’re competing for “best X near me” queries where Google has choices.

## Why you want authority (what it buys you)
For Tampa Bay Soft Wash, authority is useful because it:

- **Stabilizes rankings** (less volatility)
- **Improves lead economics** (more organic → less paid dependence)
- **Expands map footprint** (more adjacent areas)
- **Makes new pages rank faster** (pre‑trusted domain)
- **Increases conversion trust** (reviews/mentions)

## How to build authority (what works)
### A) Foundation: local entity legitimacy
- Fully optimized **Google Business Profile**
- **NAP consistency** (Name/Address/Phone) across major listings
- **Review velocity + quality** (steady stream, respond consistently)

### B) On-site topical authority
- Strong service pages + proof pages (before/after, process, FAQs)
- City pages only if they’re **unique and useful** (avoid thin clones)

### C) Earned authority: links + mentions
- **Local links:** chambers, local news, sponsorships, associations
- **Industry links:** trade/certifications/manufacturer listings
- Avoid junk link schemes; quality beats volume.

### D) Citations (still matter)
- Focus on **top-tier** directories and accuracy, not 300 random sites.

### E) Technical trust
- Speed/mobile, clean indexation
- Internal linking, schema where appropriate

## How to measure authority (without vanity metrics)
- Rankings (local pack + organic)
- GBP Insights (calls/directions)
- Organic leads (forms/calls)
- Referring domains (quality + relevance)
- Brand search lift (Google Search Console)
