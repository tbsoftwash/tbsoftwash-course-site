# Communication Timeline SOP (Warm + Professional)

**Goal:** Deliver a “top 50–75% pricing” customer experience: fast replies, clear expectations, on-time arrival, clean jobsite, documented results.

## Voice rules
- Start with **“Hey <Name>,”**
- Short, friendly sentences.
- Confirm next step + when they’ll hear from us.
- Avoid emojis in business comms (optional; use sparingly if you already do).

---

## 1) Incoming lead (web form / call / text)
**SLA target:** reply within 5–30 minutes during business hours.

### Text template — initial reply
Hey <Name>, thanks for reaching out to Tampa Bay Soft Wash.

What address are we looking at, and which services are you interested in (roof / house / windows / gutters / driveway / pool cage)?

### If they already gave address + services
Hey <Name>, thank you — I’ve got it.

I can put together a quote for <services>. Any schedule constraints we should work around?

---

## 2) Quote delivery
### Text template — sending quote
Hey <Name>, here’s your estimate for <services> at <address>: <price/range>.

This includes: <3 bullet inclusions>.

If you’d like to move forward, I can offer <2 date windows>. What works best for you?

### If quote requires site visit
Hey <Name>, for this one I’d like to do a quick walkthrough so we quote it accurately.

I can stop by <window>. Does that work?

---

## 3) Booking confirmation
Hey <Name>, you’re all set for <day/date>.

Arrival window: <time range>.

If anything changes on our end due to weather, we’ll text you as early as possible.

---

## 4) Day-before reminder
Hey <Name>, quick reminder — we’re scheduled for tomorrow (<day/date>) with an arrival window of <time range>.

If you have any special notes (gate code, pets, water spigot location), feel free to reply here.

---

## 5) “On the way” text
Hey <Name>, we’re on the way and should be there in about <X> minutes.

---

## 6) Arrival / start message (optional if you’re meeting them)
Hey <Name>, we’ve arrived.

We’ll start with a quick walkthrough + photos, then we’ll protect landscaping and begin.

---

## 7) Completion message + proof pack
Hey <Name>, we’re all finished at <address>.

Here are the before/after photos: <link or attachments>

If you’d like, I can also point out anything we noticed while we were there (maintenance items, areas to keep an eye on).

---

## 8) Payment message
Hey <Name>, whenever you’re ready, you can pay by <methods>.

If you prefer, we can send a QuickBooks invoice to <email>.

---

## 9) Review request (24–72 hours later)
Hey <Name>, thanks again for having us out.

If you were happy with the service, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps our small family business.

Link: <review link>

(And if anything isn’t perfect, please reply here — we’ll make it right.)
