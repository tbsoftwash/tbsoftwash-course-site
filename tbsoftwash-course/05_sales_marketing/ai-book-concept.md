# Book Concept: AI for Exterior Cleaning Operators

## Working titles (pick later)
1) **AI for Exterior Cleaning: The Operator’s Playbook**
2) **The AI Softwash Operator**
3) **From Pressure Washing to Process: Using AI to Scale Exterior Cleaning**

## One-line promise
A practical guide for exterior cleaning businesses to use AI to win more jobs, deliver a better customer experience, and scale operations without losing quality.

## Target reader
- Owner-operators in pressure washing/soft washing
- Small teams (1–10 techs)
- Marketing-leaning operators who want systems

## What makes it different
- Written by a real operator brand (Tampa Bay Soft Wash) with SOP mindset.
- Not generic “AI is coming” — it is **workflows, templates, and guardrails**.

## Core thesis
AI doesn’t replace craftsmanship — it replaces chaos.
It turns knowledge into repeatable SOPs, faster quoting, better communication, and consistent QA.
