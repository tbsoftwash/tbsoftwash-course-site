# Job Scope Checklist (Estimator / On-site)

## Identify surfaces
- Roof type: tile / shingle / metal / flat
- Siding/stucco type + oxidation signs
- Windows/screens
- Gutters: clean vs brightening
- Flatwork: new concrete? oil? rust?

## Risk flags
- Delicate landscaping
- Limited water access
- Steep pitch / fall risk
- Hard water / rust / oxidation

## Documentation
- Required before photos + pre-existing damage photos
- Plan for proof pack

## Upsells
- Gutter brightening
- Rust/hard water treatment
- Paver sealing
- Wood stain/seal
