# Photo Documentation Language (Copy/Paste)

## In estimate / proposal
"We document every job with before/after photos. This protects your property and ensures quality control."

## In terms & conditions
"Photos are taken before and after service for documentation, quality assurance, and to record pre-existing conditions. Photos may be used for marketing unless the customer opts out in writing."

## Opt-out line
"Please reply ‘NO PHOTOS’ if you do not want your property photos used in marketing (documentation photos will still be taken for the job record)."
