# House Washing — Homeowner Explainer (Safety + What to Expect)

This page is homeowner-safe. It explains process and expectations without chemical recipes.

## Why we use a soft-wash process
Many exterior surfaces (stucco, siding, painted trim) can be damaged by aggressive pressure.

A proper house wash is about:
- the right method for the material
- dwell time
- a controlled rinse

## Landscaping protection
We pre-wet and rinse landscaping throughout the job.

If you have sensitive plants, tell us ahead of time.

## Oxidation vs mildew (common confusion)
Sometimes a house looks “dirty,” but it’s actually oxidation (a chalky breakdown of the surface).

A standard wash can improve appearance, but oxidation may require a separate restoration process.

We’ll set expectations before we begin.

## Proof Pack
You’ll receive before/after photos so you can see exactly what changed.

## How to prepare
- Close windows and doors.
- Keep pets inside.
- Let us know about any electrical issues or delicate landscaping.
