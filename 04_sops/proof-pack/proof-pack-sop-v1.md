# Proof Pack SOP v1 (Operator)

## Service
Proof Pack creation + organization + delivery (before/during/after photos + notes) for every job.

> Operator principle: **Proof is part of the service.** If it isn’t documented, it didn’t happen.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Every job has a complete Proof Pack with consistent before/after framing.
- Pre-existing issues are documented before work starts.
- Proof Pack link is stored in the Source of Truth (CRM/job board).
- Customer receives a clean completion message with the Proof Pack link.

## Safety / PPE
- Standard jobsite PPE applies.
- Privacy/PII rules apply (no faces, no kids, avoid license plates, avoid interiors/personal items).

## Pre-checks (arrival)
- Confirm scope and identify what should be documented as pre-existing:
  - damaged screens
  - cracked tiles
  - peeling paint
  - stained/etched concrete
  - oxidized gutters

## Required shot list (minimum)
### Universal (all jobs)
1) Context shot (house number / identifiable context). Avoid people + plates.
2) Wide BEFORE (full area)
3) 2–6 close-up BEFORE shots (problem areas)
4) Wide AFTER (same framing)
5) Close-up AFTER (match each close-up)
6) Final “jobsite clean” shot (hoses coiled, area tidy)

### Roof add-ons
- Multiple roof planes + valleys
- “Worst side” (often shaded/north)
- Drip edge/gutter line
- Optional drone set (see Drone)

### Windows add-ons
- Exterior reflection check (2 angles)
- Frames/sills/tracks (if included)
- Interior protection shot (drop cloths) if inside work

### Gutters / brightening add-ons
- Tiger stripe close-up before/after
- Downspout discharge (if relevant)

### Flatwork add-ons
- Wide “striping-free” after
- Evidence of post-treat if used (operator-only details)

## Drone (optional)
- Only if conditions are safe + legal + customer ok.
- For commercial use, follow FAA rules (often Part 107).
- Standard set:
  - overhead whole-roof BEFORE
  - overhead whole-roof AFTER
  - 2–4 angled passes of worst sections

## File naming convention
**Folder:** `YYYY-MM-DD__LastName__StreetName__City__Service`

Examples:
- `2026-03-01__Peterson__RedwoodCir__Clearwater__RoofSoftWash`

**Files:**
- `01_context_before.jpg`
- `02_wide_before.jpg`
- `03_closeup_valley_before.jpg`
- `04_wide_after.jpg`
- `05_closeup_valley_after.jpg`
- `99_cleanup_after.jpg`

## Storage
- Save Proof Packs in a cloud folder by year → month.
- Retention:
  - 2 years minimum for residential
  - longer for HOA/commercial as required

## Delivery to customer (completion message)
Hey <Name>, we’re all finished at <address>.

Here are your before/after photos: <link>

Thank you again for having us out — if you have any questions or want to schedule the next maintenance visit, just reply here.

## Quality checks (QA)
- Before/after pairs match (angle/zoom as close as possible).
- No privacy issues.
- Proof Pack link is logged in CRM/job board.

## Common failure modes
- Missing BEFORE photos (crew starts cleaning too fast)
- Different angles that make the AFTER less believable
- Privacy issues (faces, plates, interiors)

## Integration

  - Review request engine
  - Content engine (GBP posts, galleries)
  - Pricing confidence (proof of value)
  - Dispute prevention (CYA)
