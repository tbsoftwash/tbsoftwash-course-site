# Window Track Detailing SOP v1 (Add-on)

## Service
Detail cleaning of window tracks/sills (interior/exterior) as an add-on.

## Goal
- Tracks visibly cleaned to scope.
- No water intrusion into interiors.
- No mess left on floors/sills.

## Safety / PPE
- Gloves.
- Drop cloths (interior).

## Tools
- Detail brush
- Microfibers
- Small vacuum (optional)
- Minimal water (spray bottle)

## Workflow
1) Protect area (drop cloths).
2) Dry remove debris first (brush/vacuum).
3) Light mist only as needed.
4) Agitate and wipe; repeat.
5) Final dry wipe.

## QA
- No standing water.
- No debris pushed into corners.

## Proof Pack
- Before/after close-up of one representative track.
