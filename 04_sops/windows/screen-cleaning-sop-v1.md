# Screen Cleaning SOP v1 (Add-on)

## Service
Window screen removal (when required), cleaning, drying, and reinstallation.

## Goal
- Screens cleaned without damage.
- No bent frames, torn mesh, or broken tabs.
- Screens reinstalled correctly.

## Safety / PPE
- Gloves (protect hands + avoid screen oils).
- Eye protection when spraying.

## Pre-checks
- Confirm which screens are included.
- Photograph any pre-existing screen tears, bent frames, broken tabs.

## Tools
- Soft brush
- Gentle spray/rinse setup
- Towels or drying rack

## Workflow
1) Remove screens carefully; label if needed.
2) Dry brush loose debris.
3) Wash gently (avoid aggressive pressure).
4) Rinse thoroughly.
5) Dry fully before reinstall.
6) Reinstall and verify fit.

## QA
- No visible debris stuck in mesh.
- Frames straight.
- Correct placement.

## Common failure modes
- Bending frames from rushing.
- Reinstalling wet screens (drips, dirt).

## Proof Pack
- Before/after of at least 1–2 representative screens.
