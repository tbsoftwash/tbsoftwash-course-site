# Water Fed Pole (WFP) Window Cleaning SOP v1 (Exterior)

## Service
Exterior window cleaning using water fed pole + purified water (RO/DI) for spot-free results.

## Goal
- Spot-free exterior glass.
- Reduced ladder time.
- Consistent results on recurring routes.

## Safety / PPE
- Eye protection.
- Awareness of overhead lines.
- Hose management to avoid trip hazards.

## Pre-checks
- Check TDS (purity) before starting.
- Confirm scope (exterior only vs add-ons).
- Identify heavy contamination (paint, concrete splatter) — may require separate process.

## Tools
- WFP + brush
- RO/DI system
- TDS meter
- Hose/fittings

## Workflow
1) Confirm TDS in acceptable range.
2) Start with frames (scrub thoroughly).
3) Scrub glass evenly.
4) Rinse with controlled technique.
5) Inspect from angles; re-hit misses.

## QA
- Spot-free check after dry-down where possible.
- Consistent technique used on all panes.

## Common failure modes
- Poor frame scrub causes drips/spots.
- High TDS causes spotting.

## Proof Pack
- Representative before/after reflection shot.
