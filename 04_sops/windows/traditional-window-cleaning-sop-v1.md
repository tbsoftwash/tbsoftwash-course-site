# SOP — Traditional Window Cleaning (Inside/Out) (v1)

> Superseded by: `04_sops/windows/traditional-window-cleaning-sop-v2.md`

## Goal
- Streak-free glass, clean frames/sills as scoped, no drips inside, customer receives Proof Pack.

## Tools
- Mop/washer + squeegee
- Scraper (only when safe)
- Microfibers
- Bucket + solution
- Drop cloths (interior)

## Workflow
1) Confirm scope: inside/out, screens, tracks
2) Protect interiors (drop cloths, shoes)
3) Dry dust/debris removal
4) Wash glass, squeegee technique, detail edges
5) Frames/sills wipe
6) Interior final check for drips
7) Proof Pack photos where possible

## QA
- No streaks in sunlight check
- No drips on sills/floors
