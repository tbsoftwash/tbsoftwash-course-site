# Flatwork Surface Cleaning SOP v2 (Operator)

## Service
Driveways, sidewalks, patios, pool decks, and other flat hard surfaces.

> Operator principle: Flatwork is where pressure washing belongs — but the goal is **consistent cleaning** (no striping), not damage.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Even, stripe-free results to the agreed standard.
- No etching or damage.
- Runoff managed responsibly.
- Proof Pack completed.

## Safety / PPE
- Eye protection.
- Hearing protection.
- Boots with traction.
- Gloves.

## Pre-checks (arrival)
### 1) Substrate matrix (decide the method)
- **Concrete (uncoated):** surface cleaner primary; avoid etching.
- **Pavers:** protect joint sand; avoid aggressive edges; consider separate sealing upsell.
- **Coated surfaces:** high risk — test first; avoid aggressive pressure.
- **Stone/soft substrates:** test; avoid damage.

### 2) Stain diagnosis
- Organic growth (algae/mildew) vs old stains (rust, paint, fertilizer, irrigation).
- Set expectations: some stains are permanent without specialty treatment.

### 3) Runoff plan
- Identify where water will flow.
- Use recovery tools where required (silt socks/berms).
- Never push dirty runoff into landscaping beds.

### 4) Pre-existing damage photos (CYA)
- cracks, spalling, etching, settled areas.

## Tools / equipment
- Pressure washer sized to surface cleaner.
- Surface cleaner.
- Edge wand.
- Optional pre-treatment applicator.
- Recovery tools when needed.

## Chemicals (ranges + why) — operator-only
No public recipes.

Operator guidance:
- Pre-treat organics to reduce pressure dependence.
- Post-treat may be used per policy.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Setup
1. Pre-wet nearby landscaping.
2. Protect adjacent walls/doors from overspray.
3. Establish runoff path.

### 2) Pre-treat (if needed)
1. Apply to organics/staining.
2. Dwell while monitoring.

### 3) Surface clean (stripe-free technique)
1. Consistent overlap passes.
2. Steady walking speed.
3. Keep surface cleaner level.
4. Avoid stopping in one place.

### 4) Edge work
1. Edges last.
2. Avoid blasting joints (pavers) and soft mortar.

### 5) Rinse + optional post-treat
1. Thorough rinse.
2. Post-treat organics if policy.

### 6) Proof Pack
Follow: `04_sops/proof-pack/proof-pack-sop-v1.md`

## Quality checks (QA)
- No striping visible from wide angle.
- Edges look clean.
- No overspray on walls/doors.

## Common failure modes
- Striping from inconsistent speed/overlap.
- Etching from too much pressure or moving too slowly.
- Joint sand loss on pavers.

## Customer communication
- Set expectations about permanent stains.
- Explain the difference between organic vs “old stains.”

## Upsells / bundles
- House wash
- Roof soft wash
- Paver sealing

## Post-job cleanup
- Final rinse of landscaping.
- Debris pickup.
- Completion notes.
