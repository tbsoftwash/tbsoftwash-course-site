# Gutter Brightening SOP v1 (Operator)

## Service
Gutter brightening / tiger-stripe removal on exterior gutters and/or soffit/fascia staining.

> Operator principle: This is a **separate service** from gutter cleaning. Brightening often involves specialty chemistry and higher risk to landscaping and finishes. Price and document accordingly.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Visible oxidation/tiger striping is reduced to the agreed standard without damaging gutter finish.
- Landscaping protected; no chemical burns.
- Proof Pack complete, including pre-existing oxidation and any areas where full restoration is not possible.

## Safety / PPE
- Sealed eye protection.
- Chemical-resistant gloves.
- Long sleeves/pants.
- Respirator if needed for fumes.

## Pre-checks (arrival)
- Confirm gutter material and finish condition.
- Identify:
  - heavy tiger striping
  - oxidation vs organic growth
  - painted vs unpainted metals
- Photograph:
  - wide runs
  - close-ups of striping
  - landscaping below

## Tools / equipment
- Application bottles/sprayers appropriate for detail work.
- Soft brushes/pads for controlled agitation.
- Dedicated rinse line.
- Ladders/stands per safety policy.
- Optional downspout control to prevent concentrated runoff.

## Chemicals (ranges + why) — operator-only
- Use purpose-built brightening chemistry based on substrate.
- Avoid public recipes.
- Always test on a small inconspicuous area.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Protect
1. Pre-wet all landscaping below work area.
2. Plan rinse cycles.

Reference: `02_chemicals/pages/landscaping-protection.md`

### 2) Test spot
1. Choose a small area.
2. Apply per product guidance.
3. Rinse and inspect.

### 3) Section work
1. Work in short sections.
2. Apply, short dwell, light agitation if needed.
3. Rinse immediately and thoroughly.

### 4) Final rinse
- Rinse all adjacent surfaces and landscaping.

### 5) Proof Pack
- Show before/after close-ups; document any permanent oxidation.

## Quality checks (QA)
- No streaking or patchiness.
- No damage to paint/finish.
- Landscaping looks healthy.

## Common failure modes
- Patchy results (inconsistent dwell/agitation)
- Finish damage (too aggressive; wrong product)
- Plant damage (insufficient pre-wet/rinse)

## Customer communication
- Explain that some oxidation may be permanent.
- Explain this is restoration work and priced accordingly.

## Upsells / bundles
- Gutter cleaning
- House wash

## Post-job cleanup
- Final rinse and site check.
- Upload Proof Pack.
