# Roof Soft Wash SOP v1 (Operator)

## Service
Roof Soft Wash (asphalt shingle, tile, metal) — exterior-only cleaning focused on organic growth removal (algae/mildew/moss/lichen) with **low pressure** methods.

> Operator principle: **Do not pressure wash roofs.** Use the right chemistry + dwell + gentle rinse. Protect landscaping. Document everything.

## Goal (definition of done)
- Roof organic growth is visibly removed to the agreed standard without surface damage.
- Surrounding property (landscaping, paint, windows, screens) is protected and left clean.
- Proof Pack complete: before/during/after photos + any pre-existing conditions documented.
- Customer receives completion message + expectations for any “light ghosting” (if applicable) and post-rain improvements.

## Safety / PPE
**Non-negotiables**
- Eye protection (sealed) + chemical-resistant gloves.
- Long sleeves/pants; consider chemical apron.
- Slip-resistant footwear.
- Respiratory protection when needed (mist/wind conditions).

**Fall safety**
- Roof work increases risk dramatically. If you can complete safely from the ground, do that.
- If roof access is required: follow ladder safety (3 points of contact), stable footing, and company policy for pitch/height limits.
- Never work a roof in lightning conditions; stop for wind gusts that compromise footing/spray control.

**Electrical**
- Identify service drop, weatherheads, solar, exterior outlets, and any exposed wiring.

**Heat / hydration**
- Follow: `04_sops/safety/heat-sun-hydration.md`

## Pre-checks (arrival)
### 1) Scope verification + photos
- Confirm roof type (shingle/tile/metal) and problem areas (north side, valleys, dormers, behind trees).
- Confirm what is **in scope** (roof only vs roof + gutters + exterior walls).
- Capture pre-condition photos:
  - wide shots (all elevations)
  - close-ups of heavy growth
  - any existing damage (cracked tiles, missing shingles, loose flashing)
  - oxidized gutters (note: “gutter brightening” is separate)

### 2) Water access + hazards
- Confirm water source + hose routing.
- Identify runoff paths + where chemical may travel (downspouts, flower beds, ponds).
- Note sensitive vegetation (hibiscus, palms, delicate annuals) and nearby surfaces.

### 3) Plant protection plan (mandatory)
- Pre-wet sensitive landscaping.
- Stage rinse hose dedicated for plant rinsing.
- Plan for continuous monitoring: plants get rinsed **before, during, and after**.

### 4) Customer expectation setting (2-minute script)
- Set “no pressure washing” safety framing.
- Explain plant protection + documentation.
- Set timeline: setup, application, dwell, rinse, final rinse.

## Tools / equipment
- Soft wash system (appropriate pump + proportioning method per your rig).
- Dedicated rinse hose/nozzle.
- Downstream/low-pressure application tools appropriate to roof type.
- Extension poles/wands as needed.
- Ladders + ladder stabilizer (as required by policy).
- Tarps/plastic for selective protection where appropriate.
- Gutter bag plugs / downspout control (when needed to protect landscaping).
- Camera/phone; optional drone for pre/post documentation (follow FAA rules and company policy).

## Chemicals (ranges + why) — **operator-only**
We do not publish exact “recipes” in public-facing materials.

Operator guidance:
- The goal is **effective dwell** on organic growth with minimal mechanical force.
- Choose strength based on:
  - roof material
  - severity/type of growth
  - shade/moisture
  - temperature/sun/wind (spray control)

**Always**
- Use surfactant/adhesion appropriate to your system so product stays where you put it.
- Control overspray; avoid drifting mist.
- Rinse plants early and often.

## Step-by-step workflow
### 1) Setup & protection
1. Stage rinse line and begin pre-wet of sensitive landscaping.
2. Cover/avoid direct application on sensitive areas when required (selective masking).
3. Confirm spray direction with wind.
4. Confirm runoff management plan.

### 2) Application (low pressure)
1. Apply evenly, working in controlled sections.
2. Focus on growth-heavy areas first (north slopes, valleys).
3. Avoid blasting seams/edges with pressure.

### 3) Dwell + monitoring
1. Allow product to dwell per field conditions.
2. Rinse plants during dwell cycles as needed.
3. Watch for signs of plant stress and respond immediately.

### 4) Rinse (as required)
1. Rinse roof appropriately for the material and product used.
2. Maintain low pressure.
3. Final rinse of gutters/downspouts and surrounding surfaces to prevent residue.

### 5) Detail / touch-ups
1. Re-apply to stubborn spots if needed.
2. Avoid repeated aggressive application in one spot without reassessing risk.

### 6) Proof Pack capture
1. Capture matching after photos from the same angles.
2. Capture “detail wins” (valleys, dormer sides, ridge line).
3. Capture any exceptions and document clearly.

## Quality checks (QA)
- Verify uniform results from ground view on all elevations.
- Check for overspray on:
  - windows
  - painted surfaces
  - screen enclosures
  - patios/pavers
- Confirm landscaping looks healthy post-final rinse.
- Confirm driveway/walkways are not left slick.

## Common failure modes
### 1) Plant damage risk
**Cause:** insufficient pre-wet/rinse; runoff concentration; wind drift.
**Prevention:** dedicated rinse person/time; runoff planning; wind control.
**Fix:** immediate dilution/rinse; document; escalate per Priority Red policy.

### 2) “Ghosting” / slow-release stains
**Cause:** heavy growth or staining; roof condition; weather.
**Prevention:** set expectations before starting.
**Fix:** document and schedule follow-up if policy allows.

### 3) Overspray on adjacent surfaces
**Cause:** wind, poor nozzle control, rushing.
**Prevention:** directional spraying, masking, staged work.
**Fix:** immediate rinse/washdown.

### 4) Tile fragility / breakage concerns
**Cause:** walking where you shouldn’t; stepping wrong; existing brittle tiles.
**Prevention:** minimize roof walking; document pre-existing conditions.
**Fix:** document, escalate to owner.

## Customer communication
### Before
- “We don’t pressure wash roofs. We use a soft-wash process designed to remove growth safely.”
- “We’re going to pre-wet and rinse your landscaping throughout the job.”
- “We’ll document before/after so you can see exactly what changed.”

### During
- Update if you see pre-existing damage.
- If customer asks about strength/chemistry: keep it safety-focused and process-based.

### After
- Completion text/email with:
  - thanks
  - what was done
  - proof photos
  - any exceptions
  - follow-up expectations

## Upsells / bundles
- Gutter cleaning
- Window cleaning
- House wash (if visible organic growth on soffits/siding)
- Gutter brightening (separate, when oxidation/tiger strip is present)

## Post-job cleanup
- Final rinse of landscaping and any overspray areas.
- Remove any coverings.
- Coil hoses; confirm no leaks/chemical left on site.
- Upload Proof Pack and log job notes.
- Trigger review request schedule per Reputation SOP.
