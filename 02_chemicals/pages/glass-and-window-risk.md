# Glass + Window Risk (Operator)

## Category
Glass is a truth serum.

If you leave residue, customers see it.

## Risks
- overspray residue on glass
- streaking from surfactant film
- hard-water deposits mistaken for “dirt”
- interior drip issues (traditional windows)

## Operator rules
- Rinse glass thoroughly when any chemical hits nearby surfaces.
- Avoid leaving surfactant residue.
- Diagnose hard-water staining and set expectations.

## Customer language
- “Hard-water staining is different from normal soil. We’ll test a small area and set expectations first.”
