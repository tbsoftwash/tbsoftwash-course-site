# Muriatic Acid (Hydrochloric Acid / HCl)

## Chemical
Hydrochloric acid — a strong acid used for specific mineral/scale tasks.

## Known as
- muriatic acid
- HCl

## Used for
- certain mineral deposits and masonry-related tasks where an acid is appropriate

## Not used for / contraindications
High risk chemical.

Not used casually.

Avoid using it as a “general cleaner.”

## Typical dilution / range (operator)
Do not publish recipes publicly.

Operator guidance:
- Treat as specialty work: test spot, short dwell, thorough rinse.
- Use the least aggressive approach that works.

## Surfactant notes
- Not typically a “cling” job; control is more important than dwell.

## Dwell time
- Short, controlled dwell.

## PPE
- Sealed eye protection
- Acid-rated gloves
- Long sleeves/pants
- Respiratory protection if fumes are present

## Storage & transport
- Store upright, secured, and ventilated.
- Keep away from incompatible chemicals.

## Neutralization / runoff
- Plan runoff.
- Protect landscaping.
- Neutralization may be required per job and local constraints.

## Safe surfaces
- Substrate-specific; only after verification and test spot.

## Avoid surfaces
- Metals and sensitive finishes unless you know exactly what you’re doing.

## Common failure modes
- Etching
- Damage to finishes
- Fume exposure
- Plant damage

## What to say to the customer (expectations)
- “This is specialty restoration work. We’ll test a small area and set expectations before proceeding.”
