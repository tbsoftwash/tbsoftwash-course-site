# Metals, Fixtures, and Corrosion Risk (Operator)

## Category
Exterior cleaning often crosses:
- light fixtures
- door hardware
- painted metal
- gutters and fasteners

## Risks
- corrosion/staining from improper rinse
- finish damage on sensitive metals
- residue on fixtures

## Operator rules
- Identify sensitive fixtures before application.
- Control overspray and drift.
- Rinse thoroughly.
- Document pre-existing corrosion.

## Customer language
- “We’ll protect and rinse fixtures throughout. If we see pre-existing corrosion, we’ll document it before we start.”
