# Sodium Metasilicate

## Chemical
Sodium metasilicate — an alkaline builder sometimes used to support cleaning/stripping tasks.

## Known as
- sodium metasilicate

## Used for
- certain cleaning/stripping support workflows (process-dependent)

## Not used for / contraindications
- Not a replacement for organic growth removal.
- Not for roofs as a default.
