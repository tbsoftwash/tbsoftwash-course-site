# Neutralization + Rinse Discipline (Operator)

## Category
Not a single chemical — this is a field discipline.

## Why it matters
Most “damage stories” in exterior cleaning come from:
- runoff concentration
- insufficient rinse cycles
- letting product dry

Neutralization and rinse discipline prevents:
- plant stress
- streaking/residue
- corrosion on sensitive metals
- angry callbacks

## The core rules
1) **Pre-wet plants before any application**
2) **Rinse throughout the job** (not just at the end)
3) **Final rinse is mandatory** (plants + hard surfaces)
4) **Control downspouts** when concentration risk exists
5) **Never let product dry** where it creates risk

## When to neutralize vs when to rinse
- Rinse is always required.
- Neutralization may be required for certain specialty acid/caustic workflows depending on surface, runoff sensitivity, and job constraints.

## Runoff control tools
- dedicated rinse hose
- downspout extensions/bags (when needed)
- selective masking

## Customer expectation language
- “We pre-wet and rinse landscaping throughout the job — it’s part of doing this safely.”

## Common failure modes
- Only rinsing at the end
- Forgetting plants on the “far side”
- Downspout dumping concentrated runoff into one bed
