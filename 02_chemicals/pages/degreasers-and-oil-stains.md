# Degreasers + Oil Stain Category (Operator)

## Category
Degreasers are used to break down oily soils (driveways, dumpster pads, commercial flatwork).

## Used for
- oil/grease on concrete
- restaurant/drive-thru zones
- shop floors (when contracted)

## Not used for / contraindications
- not a replacement for organic growth treatments
- avoid using without runoff plan

## Operator guidance (no recipes)
- Always diagnose the stain: fresh oil vs old set-in.
- Set expectations: old oil may lighten but not fully disappear.
- Use dwell + agitation where appropriate.
- Hot water can change outcomes (when available).

## Safety
- PPE (gloves/eyes) required
- runoff control required

## Customer expectation language
- “Oil stains can improve, but some are permanent. We’ll set expectations before we start.”
