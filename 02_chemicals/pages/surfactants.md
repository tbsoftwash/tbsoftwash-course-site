# Surfactants (Adhesion / Cling)

## Chemical
Surfactants are additives that help cleaning solutions:
- cling to surfaces
- spread evenly
- dwell longer

## Known as
- surfactant
- soap
- cling agent

## Used for
- improving dwell time on roofs and vertical walls
- making coverage more even

## Not used for / contraindications
- Surfactant does not replace proper diagnosis (organic vs oxidation vs rust).
- Too much surfactant can create rinsing issues/residue.

## Typical dilution / range (operator)
Follow product guidance and field-test for:
- cling
- rinse-ability
- residue risk

Avoid “more is better.”

## Surfactant notes
Key properties to evaluate:
- rinses clean on glass
- doesn’t leave film on paint
- performs in heat

## Dwell time
- Surfactant increases effective dwell by keeping solution from running off too fast.

## PPE
- Eye protection
- Gloves

## Storage & transport
- Keep sealed.
- Prevent spills (slip hazard).

## Neutralization / runoff
- Rinse thoroughly to avoid residue.

## Safe surfaces
- Most exterior surfaces when used correctly.

## Avoid surfaces
- Interiors.

## Common failure modes
- Film/residue from overuse
- “Streaking” on glass if not rinsed properly

## What to say to the customer (expectations)
- “We use a solution that clings so we can clean safely without blasting the surface.”
