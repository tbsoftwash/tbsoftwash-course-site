# How We Restore Weathered Wood — Notes

## Source
- https://tbsoftwash.com/how-we-restore-weathered-wood/

## What the post is really about
A complete “restoration sequence” framework for wood: **pre-wet → clean/strip → rinse → brighten → rinse → dry**, plus the logic behind pre-wetting and dwell discipline.

## Key points
- Defines a clear SOP and calls out why vague “trade secret” teaching is unhelpful.
- Strong emphasis on:
  - **pre-wetting** for evenness + deeper penetration (osmotic framing)
  - dwell time control; don’t let chemistry dry
  - rinse discipline; go with the grain
- Uses a comparative matrix of chemicals (cleaners/strippers/brighteners) and notes aggressiveness + risks.
- Rinsing guidance: use low-risk tips/nozzles; avoid damaging wood.
- Brightener step is framed as “wow factor” + grain pop (but situational depending on stain color).

## Signature phrases / quotable lines
- “Sometimes they are intentionally being vague as to not give away any of their ‘trade secrets’.”
- “Rinse, rinse, then rinse some more…”

## Operator translation (how we teach this safely)
- Public lane: teach **sequence + risk** (avoid publishing exact chemical ratios).
- Operator lane: convert to:
