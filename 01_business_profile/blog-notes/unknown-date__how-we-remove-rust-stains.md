# How We Remove Rust Stains — Notes

## Source
- https://tbsoftwash.com/how-we-remove-rust-stains/

## What the post is really about
A safety-forward rust removal SOP: **acid respect**, pre-wetting/runoff discipline, neutralization, and clear homeowner/operator boundaries.

## Key points
- Strong safety framing (PPE, ventilation, avoid mixing, neutralizer on hand).
- “Big box” rust removers are still acids; MSDS transparency angle.
- Lists common acids used in rust removers (oxalic most common; others more hazardous).
- Practical guardrails:
  - don’t apply to dry surfaces
  - don’t allow product to dry
  - protect vegetation; divert runoff
  - always add acid to water (not vice versa)
- Neutralization/disposal discipline: convert to safe pH before disposal.

## Signature phrases / quotable lines
- “It can also be lethal, so it needs to be treated and handled as such!”
- “Do not apply acid to dry surfaces.”

## Operator translation (how we teach this safely)
