## What’s valuable
This module is strong at:
- clear safety-first scaffolding
- equipment taxonomy (portable vs mounted, gas vs electric, belt vs direct, steam)
- measurement basics (PSI/GPM/CU) with practical constraints
- job hazard analysis prompts
- operational micro-SOPs (hoses, reels, quick connects, O-rings)
- visual instruction sequences (e.g., taping off plugs)

3) **PSI vs GPM tradeoff chart**

6) **Hose ends + rolling technique**
   - male vs female end
   - drainage + over/under coiling
7) **Quick connect anatomy + safety**
   - O-ring failure modes
   - pressure release before disconnect
