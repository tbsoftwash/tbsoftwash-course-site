# Gutter Brightening (“Whitening”) — TBSoftWash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/gutter-brightening-service/

## Positioning / what it is
- Removes black “tiger stripes” on gutter faces caused by runoff + contaminants **electrostatically bonding** to aluminum.
- Framed as curb appeal must-have; risk of falls → hire pro.

## Process (as stated)
1) Wet gutter + surrounding area (prevent clean spots + protect landscaping)
2) Gently apply brightening solution
3) Thoroughly rinse gutters + surrounding area
4) Inspection + correct any misses

## Course takeaways
- Separate module from “gutter cleaning” (different chemistry + technique).
- Emphasize pre-wet + rinse discipline to protect landscaping and prevent streaking.
- Manage expectations: different gutter colors; “whitening” terminology.
