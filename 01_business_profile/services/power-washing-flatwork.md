# Power Washing / Flatwork Pressure Cleaning — TBSoftWash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/power-washing-service/

## Positioning
- Flatwork: slabs, sidewalks, patios, decks, driveways, pavers, parking lots.
- Calls out risk of damage (pitting/lines, mortar damage, joint sand loss) if PSI not controlled.

## Frequency guidance
- Driveway: at least **annually** or when visibly dirty/overgrown; slip hazard framing.

## Important constraints
- Avoid high-pressure on concrete poured within last **12–24 months**.

## Add-ons (time/tools/heat)
- Gum, paint, oil removal = additional cost; mentions hot water unit, turbo nozzles, chemicals.

## Course takeaways
- Teach PSI/GPM/nozzle selection + “new concrete” rule.
- Make “heat vs pressure” lesson explicit for gum.
- Build commercial flatwork checklists (drive-thru lanes, dumpster pads, sport courts, etc.).
