# House Washing (Soft Wash) — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/house-washing-service/

## Positioning / promise
- “Exterior soft washing” using low pressure + solutions to remove *organic* growth (mold/algae/mildew, etc.)
- Recommends **annual** house soft wash for curb appeal.

## What’s explicitly included
- Remove organic buildup + nuisance items: wasp nests, mud dauber nests, bag worms, cob webs/spider webs.
- Landscaping protection: pre-wet, shield plants as needed.

## Organic stains called out
- Algae, mold, mildew
- Cyanobacteria
- Gloeocapsa Magma
- Lichens, moss

## Exclusions / add-ons (important for course scope + upsells)
Not included in standard soft wash packages:
- Rust/irrigation/clay stain removal (oxalic-based detergents + agitation)
- Efflorescence removal (acid-based detergents + agitation)
- Hard water spot removal (chemical → polish → replace)
- Oxidation removal (downstream specialty detergent; hot water low pressure rinse)
- Gutter brightening (separate process/products)
- French drain cleaning (jetting tip; may require repair if clog can’t be cleared)

## Commercial/HOA angles
- Positions building soft washing for shopping centers/office buildings/restaurants, schools/churches, apartments/condos, hotels.
- HOA/property management “multi-unit” list (breezeways, awnings, pool areas, garages, curbs, patios, fencing, windows, etc.).

## Course takeaways
- Teach the **organic vs mineral** stain decision tree early (what soft wash solves vs what needs acids/abrasion/heat).
- Standardize landscaping protection SOP + “no plant damage” expectation management.
- Annual maintenance program framing for recurring revenue.
