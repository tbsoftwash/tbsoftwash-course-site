# Window Cleaning — Tampa Bay Soft Wash (site extraction)
Source: https://tbsoftwash.com/window-cleaning-service/

## Positioning / tools
- Family-owned; residential + commercial.
- Emphasis on **water fed pole (WFP)** with **purified water** for spot-free finish; can also do traditional mop/squeegee.
- Safety: trained + insured for elevated heights.

## Process (as stated)
1) Remove loose debris from sills
2) Wet with purified water + scrub panes + frames/components (panes, heads, sashes, jambs, grilles, stiles, rails)
3) Rinse with purified water (spot-free)
4) Inspection + correct any misses

## Cadence
- Recommends routine cleaning; for businesses suggests **3–4x per year** for best impression.

## Exclusions / add-ons
- Hard water removal (glass corrosion; chemical → polish → replace)
- Paint overspray removal

## Course takeaways
- WFP fundamentals: purity, technique, frame cleaning, spotting troubleshooting.
- Upsell ladder: basic exterior → interior add-on → hard water/overspray restoration.
- Safety module: heights, fall protection, customer interior protection for inside cleans.
