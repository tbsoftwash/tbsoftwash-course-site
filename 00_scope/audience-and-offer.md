# Audience & Offer Definition

## Primary audience (priority)
**Operators**
- New owner-operators entering exterior cleaning (Florida-first)
- Existing pressure washing operators leveling up to soft wash + roof + systems
- Owner-operators hiring their first techs and needing SOP-based training

## Secondary audience
**Handy homeowners**
- Want to understand what "good" looks like
- Want to avoid getting scammed / hiring a hack
- May DIY small items but will hire out roofs and high-risk work

## Offer structure (recommended)
### Product 1: Free gated Operator Course (TBSoftWash Academy)
- Operators get the full SOPs + checklists + scripts
- Homeowners get “how to hire right” + safety/expectations pages

### Product 2: Paid add-on (later)
- Templates pack (proposal, QA scorecard, photo checklist pack)
- Optional book: AI disruption + AI agents for small business operators

## Success metrics
- Operator: can quote, execute, and deliver repeatable results without callbacks
- Homeowner: can evaluate a contractor confidently and understand why TBSoftWash costs more

## Content policy (safety)
- Teach chemical handling and safety.
- Publish ranges and decision logic; avoid publishing dangerous exact recipes in homeowner-facing pages.
