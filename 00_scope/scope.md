# Scope (draft)

## Objective
Create a comprehensive exterior cleaning course that captures the Tampa Bay Soft Wash business end-to-end: service delivery + sales + marketing + operations — positioned as a **top 50–75% pricing** operator (not the most expensive, but clearly above “kid with a pressure washer” pricing). Higher price point is justified by experience, attention to detail, safety, documentation, and a consistently better customer experience.

## Target learners (fill)
- New owner-operators in Florida
- Existing pressure washing operators moving into soft wash / roof
- Technicians being trained internally

## Outcomes (fill)
- Student can quote jobs confidently
- Student can execute each service safely and profitably
- Student can build repeatable SOPs and a training pipeline

## Constraints
- Florida-specific realities (humidity, algae, tile roofs, stucco, HOAs)
- Safety-first (chemicals, ladders, roof work, PPE)

## Open questions
- Primary audience: new entrants vs scaling existing?
- Desired course format: recorded, live cohort, hybrid?
- Do we include vendor-specific chemical mixes or keep ranges?
