# Review Request + Review Response SOP (Warm + Professional)

## When to ask
- Best: **24–72 hours after completion** (after they’ve enjoyed the result)
- Also good: immediately after a “wow moment” + proof pack delivery

## How to ask (principles)
- Make it easy (one link)
- Keep it short
- Ask for specifics (roof / gutters / communication / punctuality)
- Invite private feedback if anything is off

### Review request template
Hey <Name>, thanks again for having us out.

If you were happy with the service, would you mind leaving us a quick Google review? It really helps our small family business.

Link: <review link>

If anything isn’t perfect, please reply here — we’ll make it right.

---

## How to respond to reviews (principles)
- Respond to most reviews within 1–3 days
- Mention the service performed + a detail (shows it’s real)
- Keep it warm, not long

### 5-star review response template
Hey <Name>, thank you so much for the 5-star review.

We really appreciate you trusting us with your <roof/house/windows/gutters/etc.>. If you ever need anything in the future, just let us know.

— Jessica & Brian

### 4-star review response template
Hey <Name>, thank you for the review and for having us out.

If you don’t mind, what could we have done to make it a 5-star experience? We’re always trying to improve.

— Jessica & Brian

### Neutral/critical review response template (professional)
Hi <Name> — we’re sorry to hear you feel this way.

We take workmanship and property protection seriously, and we’re always happy to review concerns directly. Please call/text us at (727) 641-1296 so we can look at the details and work toward a resolution.

### If you can’t match them to a job (keep calm)
Hi <Name> — we’re not seeing a record of this job under your name.

Please call/text us at (727) 641-1296 with the service address and date so we can investigate and help.

### If review appears bad-faith / duplicate-account (do not accuse)
Hi <Name> — we’re sorry you had a negative experience.

To protect customer privacy, we handle concerns offline. Please call/text (727) 641-1296 with the service address and date so we can review our documentation and discuss next steps.

## What NOT to do
- Don’t argue facts publicly.
- Don’t mention threats, extortion, or accuse the reviewer.
- Don’t post invoices, addresses, or private messages.
