# Reviews: Voice of Customer (why Tampa Bay Soft Wash wins at top 50–75% pricing)

This doc converts Tampa Bay Soft Wash Google review language into *course-ready* operational standards.

## Core themes customers repeatedly mention

### 1) Communication + scheduling reliability
**Customer language (examples):**
- “Quick response with the quote, great scheduling. Called ahead and arrived right on time!”
- “Great communication and took extra measures…”
- “Prompt reply… showed up on time… kept great communication…”

**What this implies operationally:**
- Fast estimate response time standard (SLA)
- Clear scheduling + confirmation flow
- “On the way” call/text + arrival window discipline

**Course artifact:** Communication Timeline SOP + templates.

---

### 2) Proof + documentation (drone / before-after photos)
**Customer language (examples):**
- “The drone before and after…”
- “Before and after photos attached.”
- “Before and after pictures speak for themselves.”

**What this implies operationally:**
- Standard photo set on every job
- Consistent angles + lighting + naming
- Optional drone workflow for roofs

**Course artifact:** Proof Pack Checklist + file naming + storage.

### 3) Professionalism + trust (family-run, respectful, courteous)

**What this implies operationally:**

- Site etiquette + respect for property
- Calm, simple education without lecturing

**Course artifact:** “Premium Demeanor” mini-module + scripts.

### 4) Attention to detail + ‘go the extra mile’ behavior
**Customer language (examples):**

### 6) Value framing (not cheapest; worth it)
**Customer language (examples):**

**What this implies operationally:**
- Price is defended by proof + process + reliability

**Course artifact:** Value Stack slide + estimate script.

## Reputation management SOP (professional, top-of-market posture)

### Principles
- Never argue in public.
- Be brief, calm, and policy-based.
- Invite offline resolution.
- Refer to documentation (photos, scope, tests) without dumping details.

### Negative review response template (professional)
> Hi <Name> — we’re sorry to hear you feel this way. We take workmanship and property protection very seriously.
> 
> We document our jobs with before/after photos, follow a surface-appropriate process, and we’re always willing to review any concerns directly. Please call/text us at (727) 641-1296 so we can look at the details and work toward a resolution.

### Suspected bad-faith / duplicate-account situation (still professional)
> Hi <Name> — we’re sorry you had a negative experience. We take claims of property damage seriously and we do not ignore them.
> 
> To keep customer information private, we handle these matters offline. Please contact us at (727) 641-1296 with the job address/date so we can review our documentation and discuss next steps.

(Notes: do not accuse. Do not mention “threats” unless absolutely necessary; keep it unemotional.)
