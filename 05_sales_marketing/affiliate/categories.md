# Amazon Gear Coverage Categories (Everything Possible)

## Operator (field)
- PPE: gloves, eye protection, respirators, boots
- Plant protection: pre-wet tools, neutralizers, tarps
- Property protection: tape, outlet covers, lock covers, cones
- Soft wash systems: pumps, proportioners, manifolds, injectors
- Hoses/fittings: reels, swivels, quick connects, clamps
- Pressure washing: surface cleaners, nozzles, tips
- Window cleaning: WFP, brushes, DI resin, squeegees
- Ladders/safety: stabilizers, harness components (where appropriate)
- Measurement: laser measure, camera/drone accessories

## Operator (ops)
- CRM/invoicing (where available)

- clipboards/checklists
- vehicle organization

- outdoor furniture covers
- plant watering tools
- hose timers
- basic PPE for watching/prep (gloves/eye protection)
- contractor-evaluation checklist downloads (not a product, but can include a binder/notebook)

- lav mic

- SD cards/storage

## AI/automation (operator)
- microphones/headsets
- dictation accessories
- optional: mini PCs / SBCs (edge nodes) where relevant
