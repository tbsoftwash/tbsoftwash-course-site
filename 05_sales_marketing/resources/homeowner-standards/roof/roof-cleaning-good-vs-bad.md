# Roof Cleaning: Good vs Bad Practices (Homeowner Standards Guide)

## The safe truth
Most roofs should be cleaned with **low pressure** (soft wash), not high pressure.

## Good practices (what you should see)
- Clear explanation of the method (soft wash for most roofs)
- Plant protection (pre-wet, rinse, runoff control)
- Wind/overspray awareness
- Before/after documentation (Proof Pack)
- Honest expectations (some staining improves over time)

## Bad practices (red flags)
- High-pressure blasting shingles
- No plant/runoff protection
- “One price for everything” with no inspection
- No documentation

## What to expect
- Your roof should look better immediately, and sometimes continues improving over days.
