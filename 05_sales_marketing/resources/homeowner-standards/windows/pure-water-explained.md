# Windows (Homeowner Lane): Pure Water (WFP) Explained

## What it is
Pure water window cleaning uses purified water (low dissolved minerals) so windows can dry spot-free.

## Why there’s no soap
Soap can leave residue.

Pure water relies on:
- water quality
- agitation (brush)
- thorough rinse

## When it’s the best choice
- multi-story exteriors
- recurring maintenance

## When we switch methods
If a window has:
- heavy hard water staining
- frame oxidation risk

We may switch to traditional methods or recommend separate restoration.

## The honest promise
We’re not guessing.

We test, choose the safest method, and set expectations.
