# Proof Pack for Property Managers (What “Good” Looks Like)

Property managers don’t just want a clean surface. They want confidence:
- that the work was completed
- that the property was respected
- that there’s documentation if someone complains

## What we include (baseline)
- before photos (wide + problem areas)
- after photos (matching angles)
- exception notes (pre-existing damage, permanent stains, oxidation limits)
- completion message

## Why this matters
Documentation reduces:
- disputes
- callbacks
- vendor churn

It also protects your schedule. A property that is cleaned on a documented rhythm stays easier to maintain.
