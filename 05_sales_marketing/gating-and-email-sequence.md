# Gating + Email Sequence (Operator-first)

## Gating model (recommended)
- Academy pages: mostly ungated for SEO + trust.
- Operator Pack downloads: **gated**.

### Primary lead magnet
**Operator Checklist Pack (Free)**
Includes:
- Communication Timeline SOP (templates)
- Proof Pack SOP
- Quote intake checklist
- QA scorecard template
- Roof measurement cheat sheet (Google Earth + pitch factor)

### Secondary lead magnet
**Homeowner Hiring Checklist Pack (Free)**
Includes:
- Contractor screening checklist
- Soft wash vs pressure wash questions
- What to expect on day-of service

## Email sequence (operators) — 5 emails
### Email 1: Delivery + quick start
- Link to pack
- “Start with Roof SOP + Proof Pack”
- CTA: reply with your market + services

### Email 2: Method selection framework
- organic vs mineral vs physical contamination
- “pressure is not the solution”

### Email 3: Price justification (top 50–75% model)
- documentation, plant protection, QA, communication
- “why we cost more than the kid with a washer”

### Email 4: Operational systems
- scheduling, reminders, on-the-way text
- reducing no-shows and complaints

### Email 5: Case study + CTA
- before/after + review excerpt
- CTA: book an estimate / follow Tampa Bay Soft Wash / share

## Email sequence (homeowners) — 3 emails
1) Delivery + what to expect
2) Questions to ask a contractor
3) Proof pack + review request (how to judge quality)
