# Proof Pack SOP (Before/After Photos + Delivery)

**Purpose:** Proof is part of the service. It increases perceived value, reduces disputes, supports premium pricing, and drives reviews/referrals.

## Standards
- Every job gets a Proof Pack.
- Photos are taken **before** any visible change is made (before pre-wet/brush if possible).
- Same angle, same zoom, same framing for before/after.
- If lighting changes, note it (clouds/sunset).

## Required shot list (minimum)
### Universal (all jobs)
1) House number / identifiable context shot (no people, avoid license plates)
2) Wide “before” (shows the full area)
3) 2–6 close-ups of problem areas (stains, algae, tiger stripes, mud dauber nests, etc.)
4) Wide “after” (same as #2)
5) Close-up “after” (match each close-up)
6) Final “jobsite clean” shot (no mess, hoses coiled, area tidy)

### Roof cleaning add-ons
- Ridge line + multiple roof planes
- “Worst side” (usually shaded/north)
- Gutter line / drip edge areas
- Optional: drone set (see Drone section)

### Windows add-ons
- Exterior glass reflection check (2 angles)
- Frames/sills/tracks (if included)
- Interior protection shot (drop cloths if inside)

### Gutters / brightening add-ons
- Tiger stripe close-up before/after
- Downspout discharge (if relevant)

### Flatwork add-ons
- Wide “striping-free” after (avoid lines)
- Post-treat evidence if used

## Drone SOP (optional)
- Only if conditions are safe + legal + customer ok.
- Standard set:
  - 1 overhead “whole roof” before
  - 1 overhead “whole roof” after
  - 2–4 angled passes highlighting worst sections

## File naming convention
**Folder:** `YYYY-MM-DD__LastName__StreetName__City__Service`
Examples:
- `2026-02-27__Peterson__RedwoodCir__Clearwater__RoofSoftWash`

**Files:**
- `01_context_before.jpg`
- `02_wide_before.jpg`
- `03_closeup_northplane_before.jpg`
- `04_wide_after.jpg`
- `05_closeup_northplane_after.jpg`
- `99_cleanup_after.jpg`

## Delivery to customer (message template)
Hey <Name>, we’re all finished at <address>.

Here are your before/after photos: <link>

Thank you again for having us out — if you have any questions or want to schedule the next maintenance visit, just reply here.

## Storage
- Save Proof Packs in a cloud folder by year → month.
- Keep at least:
  - 2 years for standard residential
  - longer for HOA/commercial (as required)

## Privacy rules
- No faces, no kids.
- Avoid license plates.
- If interior work: avoid personal photos/documents.
