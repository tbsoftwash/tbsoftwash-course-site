# SEO Keyword Ranking Sheets — What They Are + Why They Matter

This folder contains **four different keyword ranking sheets**. Each sheet answers a different business question.

> Important: **"Most searched" is not automatically "most profitable"**.
> Search volume measures demand. Profit comes from **intent + fit + conversion rate**.

---

## 1) Volume Rank (Demand)
**Question it answers:** *What do the most people search for?*

**Why it matters:**
- Builds your **site architecture** (pillar pages + subpages).
- Helps you decide which services deserve the most content investment.
- Good for top-of-funnel traffic and brand visibility.

**Risk if you only use this sheet:**
- You can attract a ton of low-intent DIY traffic and get fewer calls.

---

## 2) Buyer Intent Rank (Leads)
**Question it answers:** *Which searches are most likely to become phone calls / quotes?*

**Why it matters:**
- Prioritizes terms with purchase intent signals:
  - “near me”, city names
  - “company”, “service”, “contractor”
  - “quote”, “estimate”, “pricing”, “cost”
- Produces **revenue-first** pages (service + city + pricing intent).

**Risk if you skip this sheet:**
- You may rank but not convert.

---

## 3) CPC / Commercial Value Rank (Market Price Signal)
**Question it answers:** *What keywords are advertisers willing to pay for?*

**Why it matters:**
- CPC is a crude but useful proxy for **commercial value**.
- Helps prioritize pages that can lower paid spend by ranking organically.
- Helps pricing/offer thinking (markets pay more for certain services).

**Risk if you skip this sheet:**
- You may spend time on content that’s cheap/low-value in your market.

---

## 4) Local Opportunity Rank (Your Reality)
**Question it answers:** *Where are we already close to winning?*

**Why it matters:**
- Uses your real data (Google Search Console + GBP):
  - high impressions + low CTR → quick title/meta wins
  - positions 4–15 → easiest SEO gains
- Fastest ROI: improve what’s already working.

**Risk if you skip this sheet:**
- You waste time building new pages while easy wins sit untouched.

# Notes on data quality
Keyword Planner values can be bucketed (e.g., showing very large rounded values). Use these ranks as directional, then validate with:
- Google Search Console (what you *actually* get impressions/clicks for)
- GBP insights (local discovery behavior)
