# 04 — Local Opportunity Rank (Your Reality)

**Purpose:** rank keywords by **how close you already are to winning** using your real performance data.

## Data sources
- **Google Search Console** (queries + pages + position + impressions + CTR)
- **Google Business Profile** (calls, direction requests, discovery queries)

## What we prioritize
- **High impressions + low CTR** → fix titles/meta first
- **Avg position 4–15** → easiest SEO wins
- **Queries mapping to the wrong page** → internal linking + page targeting fixes
