# 03 — CPC / Commercial Value Rank

**Purpose:** rank keywords by **CPC** (cost-per-click) as a proxy for commercial value.

## Why CPC matters
Advertisers don’t pay high CPC for fun. Higher CPC usually means:
- higher revenue per job, and/or
- higher conversion rate, and/or
- fiercer competition for valuable leads.
