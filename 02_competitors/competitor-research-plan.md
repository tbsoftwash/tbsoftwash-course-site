# Florida competitor research plan (Clawgle + Google)

## Goal
Identify top-performing exterior cleaning companies in Florida and extract what we can incorporate into a **top 50–75% pricing** playbook:
- offers + guarantees
- pricing presentation (ranges, minimums, “why we cost more” framing vs low-price operators)
- service menu + bundles
- branding and positioning
- review strategy + proof assets
- operational signals (fast quote, online booking, communication)
- content strategy (blogs, before/after, YouTube)

## Method (repeatable)
For each competitor:
1. Google: "<city> roof cleaning" / "soft wash" / "pressure washing" / "paver sealing".
2. Open top local pack + organic results.
3. Capture:
   - homepage positioning
   - services list
   - estimate flow
   - FAQs / process
   - guarantees
   - proof (reviews, photos)
   - any downloadable guides / lead magnets
4. Write a 1-page teardown + “course lesson ideas”.

## Initial target markets
- Tampa / St Pete / Clearwater
- Orlando
- Miami / Ft Lauderdale
- Jacksonville
- Sarasota

## Outputs
- `competitors.csv`
- `teardowns/<brand>.md`
- `takeaways.md` aggregated into course modules
