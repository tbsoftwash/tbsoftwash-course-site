# Competitor Teardown — Spot Free Roofs and Pressure Cleaning, Inc.

- Website: https://spotfreeroofs.com
- Primary markets: Clearwater/Tampa Bay

## Positioning (what they claim)
- “Your premiere roof cleaning and pressure cleaning specialists”
- Family-owned
- “Giving 110%” mission statement
- Emphasizes humid coastal climate specialization
- Eco-friendly angle: “biodegradable detergents” + “low-pressure soft wash approach”

## Offer stack
- Roof cleaning (soft-wash roof cleaning)
- Pressure cleaning (driveways)
- Exterior walls

## Estimate flow (friction audit)
- Strong CTA: “Call us at 727-678-9604”
- Simple phone-first estimate pitch

## Proof system
- Mentions transformation and customer satisfaction; page content extracted did not show a structured proof pack system.

## Content strategy
- Homepage copy heavy; not obviously SEO-structured in extraction.

## Operational signals
- “Fast, reliable, professional”
- Emphasis on professional service

## What we should steal (Tampa Bay Soft Wash integration)
- Climate specificity: “humid coastal climate” framing.
- Eco-friendly “tough yet gentle” phrasing (align with soft-wash safety).

## What we should avoid
- Generic superlatives without proof artifacts.

## Course lesson ideas
- Module: Positioning + scripts → writing climate-specific “why soft wash” copy.
- SOP tie-in: Proof Pack should be explicit and consistent.
