# Module 11 — Ops: Source of Truth + Closeout Discipline (Lesson)

## The rule
If it isn’t logged, it didn’t happen.

## What we mean by “Source of Truth”
One place where the job status and key notes live:
- customer name + address
- service(s)
- schedule
- price
- Proof Pack link
- exceptions (pre-existing damage, limitations)

## Closeout discipline (same day)
At minimum, every job gets:
- Proof Pack link
- completion message sent
- status updated

## Why this matters
- prevents disputes
- powers reviews
- powers content
- protects premium pricing
