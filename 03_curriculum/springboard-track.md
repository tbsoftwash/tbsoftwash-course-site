# Springboard Track — Gutter → Traditional Windows → WFP/RODI

## Why this sequence works
- Gutters: low cost, high need, easy to upsell brightening/roof/drains
- Traditional windows: teaches detail + customer experience, low equipment cost
- WFP/RODI: premium upgrade, improves safety + speed, enables maintenance plans
