# Windows (Homeowner Lane): Screens + Tracks (Expectations)

## The difference
- Glass cleaning makes the view clear.
- Screens and tracks make the whole window feel “finished.”

## Why screens matter
Dirty screens drip back onto clean glass.

## Why tracks matter
Tracks collect:
- pollen
- dust
- grime

Track detailing is labor and often a separate scope.

## How to avoid disappointment
When booking, choose:
- glass only
- glass + screens
- glass + screens + tracks

## What we’ll document
If we see damage or seal failure, we’ll document it before we start.
