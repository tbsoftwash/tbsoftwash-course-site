# Module 9 — Sales: Speed-to-Lead + Intake (Lesson)

## The rule
Leads are melting ice cubes.

If you aren’t first, you’re last.

## SLA targets (realistic)
- Business hours: reply within **5–30 minutes**
- After-hours: reply first thing the next morning

## Bare-bones intake (the 5-point filter)
You need only enough info to:
- quote accurately
- avoid bad surprises
- route to the right service lane

Ask:
1) Address
2) Services requested
3) Timeline (how soon)
4) Access notes (gate, water)
5) Anything sensitive (plants, pets)

## Output
- Either book a walkthrough OR request photos OR send a quote.

## Proof matters
Let customers feel your professionalism before the truck arrives:
- “We’ll do a quick walkthrough + photos, then protect landscaping and begin.”
