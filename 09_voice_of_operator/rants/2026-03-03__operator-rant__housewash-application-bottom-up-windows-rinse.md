## 1) Raw notes (lightly cleaned)

- When you apply soft wash solution, you apply from the **BOTTOM up**, then work your way up.
  - Why: if you start at the top, solution runs down and as water evaporates (especially in sun/heat), it can effectively concentrate and leave drip marks / “clean spots” that show as streaks.

- Rinsing is always done **top-down**.

- Dirt dobber / mud wasp nest stains can often be cleaned with **oxalic acid** + a gentle scrub using a cobweb brush on a pole.
  - Caution: do not scrub heavily oxidized surfaces unless the customer understands the risk.

- Windows must be rinsed heavily and frequently. Do not let solution dry on glass.

- Promote roof washes when you see a dirty roof (and HOA customers often have roof cleanliness rules).

- Downstream (DS) is the recommended tool for application.
  - 12V pumps can work to start, but they can be inefficient for rinsing and get overworked. Don’t “forever-12V” your business.

- Every time SH is used: test paint colors. Running theme.
  - Even roof work: overspray/runoff can affect paints that use organic pigments.

- Bodycam / Meta glasses can help with liability and proof of work.

---

## 2) Course-ready insert lines (public-safe)

- Apply **bottom-up**, rinse **top-down**.
- Prevent streaks: don’t let solution run and dry on hot siding.
- Glass discipline: rinse windows early and often.
- Paint discipline: test first, especially on darker colors and unknown coatings.
- Tool progression: DS is the default; 12V is a starting tool, not the endgame.
- Optional: bodycam for proof + disputes.
